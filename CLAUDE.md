# Clarity Portal Codebase Guide

**Working folder (from 26 Oct):** `~/code/clarity-portal`, outside OneDrive, so syncing never touches `.git` or `node_modules` mid-change. Start Claude Code there with `cd ~/code/clarity-portal && claude`. GitHub is the backup. The old copy under OneDrive (`Client Portal/clarity-portal`) is a fallback for one week only; never work in it.

## Design rules (Clarity Portal)

1. **Dark and light modes both exist**, chosen as Light, Dark or Match my device (the starting choice), in My Account under Appearance. **Every page follows the choice** (Tim, 10 Nov), the older pages not yet rebuilt included: nothing in the portal is held in dark any more (`src/lib/colourMode/boot.js`). The outside Health Check (`/health-check`) has no Appearance setting, so it follows the person's device, light or dark (Tim, 10 Nov, 23:22): its own page in its own styles (`src/health-check/hc.css`), and the portal's own route through the same boot file, whatever was chosen for the portal on that device. It is checked in both modes by `tests/phase5/calm-rules.mjs`. Every colour comes from a token with a dark and a light value, so check any page you touch in both.

2. **Never hard-code a colour, font, font size, spacing or timing.** All design values must come from `src/styles/tokens.css` (CSS variables starting with `--`) and all component classes from `src/styles/components.css`. This includes button styles, borders, text sizes, padding, gaps, animation duration, and motion curves.

3. **No gradients anywhere in the interface.** Buttons, borders, backgrounds and other elements use flat single colours only. The only gradient allowed anywhere is inside the supplied logo image files (which are never modified).

4. **Nekst capitals only for page titles, section names and buttons.** Always uppercase with wide letter spacing, never below 10px, never in paragraphs. Small labels, question headings and everything else are DM Sans in sentence case (paragraphs, descriptions, typed answers, drop-down options and inputs; inputs 16px minimum). No overlines.

5. **Coral (#FF6F5C) is for actions and the active tab. Mint (#5FEFBB, deep green #0B7A52 in light) means good: success, saved, done, and data at or above the standard (see the 26 Oct design rules).** Buttons use black text (#000000) on flat coral. The button classes are `.ui-btn-primary` (filled coral) and `.ui-btn-ghost` (outlined). Use var(--coral), var(--good) and var(--mint-text) only.

6. **The standard crosshair (Crosshair, cropped from the logo) is used only, turned 45 degrees, for close.** Never as a plus: add buttons are words only, and steppers use the plain minus and plus (`StepIcon`: 16px, 1.5px lines, round ends; 5e rules 29e and 29g, swept on 10 Nov). Anything that opens a list or a folded section (drop-downs, folds, expandable rows, the date box) uses DisclosureArrow, one half of the same crosshair crop, pointing down and turning to point up while open; back and next use the same chevron turned (Chevron, 26 Oct design rule 6). Two exceptions: the turning crosshair of the loading spinner, and the breathing orb's 8px crosshair. The bold crosshair (BoldCrosshair) is used only for the Home Screen icon, the favicon, the desktop menu button, the phone bar's centre button and every PDF footer (Tim, 26 Oct, 16:13), always drawn from its one shared drawing (`src/lib/boldCrosshair.js`). The crosshair is never smaller than 14px apart from the orb, and never redrawn.

7. **Page titles use the shared PageHeader** (src/components/design): the title, a quiet meta line, and any explanation behind the small "i". No overline and no intro line under the title. Each page's explanation lives in src/content/pageText.js and shows as one line behind the "i".

8. **Use the word Mission everywhere, never Purpose, always with a capital M** (Tim, 10 Nov, 23:22): "Edit my Mission", "Save my Mission", "What’s your Mission?", "Your Mission isn’t written yet.", matching the My Mission pill. Never "my mission" or "your mission" in anything people read (code names, addresses and database values keep theirs; the legal text's "mission statement" waits for the lawyer's draft).

8a. **Edges people rely on reach 3 to 1** (design reference: "Box edges"; Tim, 11 Nov). An answer row at rest, a row that opens levels, a dashed empty day box and the outside Health Check's outlined buttons and answer rows all take the edges colour (`--field-line`; `--edge` on the Health Check's own page: #636362 in dark, #858480 in light), never the fainter line colours. Hover is a step stronger (`--text-2`), chosen is the text colour. Measured by `tests/phase5/answers2.mjs`. **Every outlined edge does** (Tim, 12 Nov): outlined buttons (`.ds-btn--outline`, so the Add button too; the older `.ui-btn-ghost`, `.auth-btn-ghost` and `.dng`), the Breathe and chat buttons, the segmented control's outer edge, chips (the journal's prompts), a guided tool's current-step pill, and the older pages' text boxes and steppers (`.plan-input`, `.tsa-stepper`) all take `--field-line`. `--line`, `--btn-line` and `--outline` are for lines between rows and for chart marks, never for the outline of something to tap or type into. Checked on every page by calm-rules (`tests/phase5/edges.mjs` reads every outline) and by `tests/phase5/answers3.mjs`.

8b. **Windows high-contrast mode (forced colours; Tim, 12 Nov).** A masked icon (the crosshair, the chevron, a tick) is a flat colour seen through a mask, and that mode replaces the colour, so each one sets `forced-color-adjust: none` and the system's text colour (`CanvasText`) under `@media (forced-colors: active)`: `design.css`, `components.css` and the Health Check's `hc.css`. A new masked icon needs the same. Checked by `tests/phase5/answers3.mjs`.

9. **Spacing uses only the scale --space-1 to --space-7 (4px, 8px, 16px, 24px, 32px, 48px, 80px).** Tap targets are at least 44px (var(--tap-min)). No arbitrary spacing values.

10. **Motion uses only the --ease curve and the --dur-fast (150ms), --dur (250ms) and --dur-slow (400ms) timings.** Animate only transform, opacity, colour and size. No bounce, spin or fly-in effects. Everything switches off under prefers-reduced-motion. Planned movements include: drawer slide with scrim fade, crosshair rotation when a section opens, smooth expand of the Your Mission pill, sliding highlight in segmented controls and tab bar, switch knob glide, modal fade and rise, dropdown fade, button hover lift, cross-fade between dark and light, page fade between routes.

11. **Logo files in public/brand are used as supplied and never redrawn, recoloured, stretched or cropped.** The files are lockup-full_colour-on-dark.png (full logo with wordmark for desktop header), mark_colour-on-dark.png (emblem/mark for phone), and crosshair_white.png / crosshair_black.png (mask images for CSS masks).

    The logo mark always includes its crosshair. Light backgrounds: Emblem Logo POSITIVE (black crosshair, `public/brand/logomark-positive.png`). Dark backgrounds: Emblem Logo NEGATIVE (white crosshair, `public/brand/logomark-negative.png`).

12. **Home is Today (mock-up M2; Phase 4 step 2), at /welcome.** (The daily tools' names are those of rule 26a.) Top to bottom: the greeting (or "Good to see you" after a gap of 7 days with nothing logged, planned or reviewed, `GAP_DAYS` in `src/lib/performance/home.js`; "Welcome back" on the first day back from a Recovery), the mini dial with the date and "Week 4 of 8", one Suggested for now card, at most two quiet lines, "Carry on where you left off" (`src/lib/carryOn.js`), Today (how you want to be today, today's Strategy, the day's to-dos with the highest priority first, Daily plan and Daily review links, Add a to-do, Log today with the daily habits and the weekly ones folded into one This week line), the week line (performance score and usual range), then "Get help with something" (M2 drew "I need help with something"; never "I" on a button, writing rule 13). The one suggestion comes from `homeSuggestion` in `src/lib/performance/home.js`: Daily plan until it is done or 12:00, then "How is today going?", Daily review from 17:00, the debrief on debrief days from 12:00, what comes next in the sprint's last week, Sleep wind down from 21:00 to 03:00 on the person's own clock (Not tonight hides it for that evening on every device: `person_settings.page_preferences.wind_down_not_tonight`). Logging and to-dos on Home are the sprint page's own (`src/pages/performance/sprintActions.js`, the sprint's copy on the device), never a second way to save. In Recovery Home shrinks to the recovery day's basics (M15). Log today, Daily plan, Daily review and Add a to-do are never more than two taps from Home. Data that doesn't exist yet (coach notes, coaching sessions, the first check-in, agreements) shows nothing.

13. **My Account is in the calm layout (10 Nov; design reference version 28).** Sections with the line above: Profile, My Mission (its Edit opens the plan's own Mission panel, so there is one Mission; the switch "Show my Mission at the top of every page" is the shared `SwitchRow`, with no word beside it), Appearance (`ColourModeChoice`), Settings (the sleep target range, Customise my dashboard, archived plans only when there are some, About), Sign out, and Delete my account. Quiet text links, never big outlined buttons; no "Danger zone".

14. **Design tasks must not change logic, routing, database code or ProtectedLayout.jsx.** Design work happens on a feature branch and is merged to main only when explicitly asked. Every task ends with a summary in the specified format.

15. **The breathing tool (Breathe) is the showpiece of the portal.** Three techniques: Physiological sigh, Vagal breathing, Box breathing. A ring that swells and settles with faint echo rings for the first two techniques. For Box breathing, a coral line traces the rounded-square outline of the logo mark, one side per phase. The ring is coral on the in-breath and mint on the out-breath. The crosshair sits in the centre and rotates 45 degrees as the ring grows. All timings come from the Breathing Settings.

16. **Navigation (Phase 4, mock-up M3).** The menu never lists tools: it lists areas, and each area opens a hub page of type-only tiles (`HubTile`: the tool's name, one line on how it helps, how long it takes). Desktop: the bold crosshair in the header opens the slim menu from the left (`DesktopMenu`; menu-options.html option A, Tim, 9 Nov): a 280px panel in the body font at 15px, with Pinned (up to 3), Home, then Dashboard, Plan, Reset and Review under the quiet label Personal, Interpersonal and Strategic under With others, and My Account and Sign out at the bottom below a line (`DESKTOP_MENU` and `menuCurrent` in `src/lib/navigation.js`). No Log today or Breathe in the desktop menu: Log today is on Home and Breathe has its own button. Phone: no hamburger and no header menu button; the phone bar (`PhoneBar`) has Home, the bold crosshair (opens `TileMenu` from the bottom: Pinned, the four area tiles, Log today and Breathe) and This week (the Performance Sprint). Hubs: `/personal` (Plan, Reset and Review; the Dashboard joins in Phase 5), `/personal/plan`, `/personal/reset`, `/personal/review`, `/interpersonal` and `/strategic` (the Health Check first). My Account holds About and Log out. The map is one file, `src/lib/navigation.js` (tools, hubs, menu areas, old routes); only tools that exist get a tile, and a hub ⓘ starts with one full sentence saying what the area is (`src/content/pageText.js`). Pins (`PinPanel`, "Pin tools to my menu" at the foot of each hub but Personal) are saved in `person_settings.page_preferences.menu_pins` through the offline queue, with the device copy shown first (`src/lib/menuPins.js`). Old section pages (`/plan-section`, `/planning-tools`, `/decide-section`, `/decision-tools`, `/ground-section`, `/review-section`, `/account-section`) and `/points-of-leverage` redirect to their hub with the query, hash and state passed on (App.jsx `MovedTo`). Every bar and menu edge stays inside the safe area.

17. **Every page sits inside .ui-root.** Never put global rules on html, body, #root or bare element selectors (input, button, textarea, select). (Two exceptions: the root text size in `src/index.css`, so text follows the device's text-size setting; and the safe-area insets on #root in `src/pwa-standalone.css`, so nothing sits under the notch or the Dynamic Island: the sides on every touch screen, all four as a Home Screen app. The top bar adds the top inset itself in the browser.) All design variables (--bg, --text, --coral, etc.) and component classes live only inside .ui-root and its data-theme variants. Everything a page opens, including pop-ups, panels, toasts and overlays, renders inside .ui-root too: if it attaches to document.body, give that element the .ui-root class so it sees the design variables.

18. **Every free-text box uses the shared steady text box (TextBox in src/components/design)**, with focus mode for long writing on phone. It grows to show all of its text with no hidden text, no scrolling inside the box and no max-height cap; the page scrolls instead, and the page never jumps while typing. AutoGrowTextarea is retired.

19. **Database timestamps are UTC with no time-zone ending on the live database.** Always read a saved created_at/updated_at (or similar) through parseDbTimestamp (src/lib/dateFormatter.js) before turning it into a Date, formatting it, sorting it or comparing it - never new Date(value) directly. Never use toISOString().split('T')[0] to get "today"'s date; use getLocalDateKey instead, which is built from the device's own getFullYear/getMonth/getDate. Times are shown on a 24 hour clock with no am or pm (07:53, 19:26) in the device's local time, always through formatClockTime or formatDayMonthTime in src/lib/dateFormatter.js - nothing formats a time on its own.

20. **Tables and grids must never squash.** Use fixed column widths, keep headings to one line, let cell text wrap (overflow-wrap: anywhere) and let rows grow to fit their content - never let a column crush its text or force a sideways scroll. Stack into cards under ~600px instead of shrinking a table.

21. **No swear words anywhere.** Not in product text, not in file names, not in code comments, not in variable or component names. If one is ever found, rename/reword it immediately.

22. **Mock-up files are the source of truth.** Before building or changing any page, open and read the actual mock-up file and spec in full. Never build from a summary or from memory. Copy markup, CSS values, wording and behaviour from the mock-up. If the built page differs, the mock-up wins. If something cannot match, stop and report it instead of simplifying. Every build summary must list the files read and any differences left.

23. **Wording and spelling: always write The Clarity Portal with a capital T on The, everywhere it is named.** Never "the Clarity Portal" or "a Clarity Portal account", even mid-sentence, in headings, buttons, legal text, emails, aria-labels, titles and toasts. Code identifiers, file names, CSS classes, URLs, environment variables and database values are not changed.

24. **The eye means "who can see this".** Anything personal shows the shared WhoSees label (src/components/design): a 16px line eye (1.5px lines) in the colour of its words, then 14px words in the secondary grey (26 Oct design rule 5): ("Only you", "You and [coach's first name]", "Your team"), with "Who can see this:" for screen readers. The eye is the only who-sees icon; nothing uses a lock for who sees something.

25. **There is one "i", drawn the same everywhere** (InfoIcon in src/components/design): a 16px outlined circle with a small "i" inside, like ⓘ, drawn with 1.5px lines in the current text colour, with no disc behind it. Its button gives the 44px tap area with padding; the icon never grows. When an "i" explains a sentence it sits at the end of that sentence, 6px after the last word (var(--info-gap)), lined up with the middle of the text. It opens its explanation and Escape closes it.

26. **Names people see (Tim, 23 Oct).** The personal area once called Ground is **Reset** everywhere it shows. GROW is **Get unstuck**, and its first screen says "Uses the GROW model." in small text under the title. Inversion is **Pre-mortem** (the team version for strategy stays "Pre-mortem for an initiative" if it exists). Tools not built yet take their new names when they are built. URLs now follow the new names too (Phase 4, step 5): /get-unstuck, /pre-mortem, /pressure-plan, /time-audit, /give-difficult-feedback and /life-balance-review, each with its steps under it. Every old URL redirects to its new one with its query, hash and state (`RENAMED` in `src/lib/navigation.js`), so saved items, pins, bookmarks, old emails and Home Screen apps still open the same thing; /plan-my-day and /review-plan redirect only when no saved plan is being opened. File names, database values and saved tool types keep their old names, and old saved items still open.

26a. **Names in a daily, weekly, monthly set (Tim, 10 Nov).** Plan my day is the **Daily plan** and Review my day is the **Daily review**; the Weekly performance debrief keeps its name; the Hardware and Software check-in is the **Monthly Hardware and Software review** ("Monthly review" where space is tight, and as the title on screen); a coaching client's First check-in is the **First review**. Saved items read "Today’s plan", "Yesterday’s plan", then "Daily plan" (reviews the same, then "Daily review"), "November review" for a monthly one, and "First review". Home's line is "Your monthly review is ready." Buttons that start them read "Start my daily plan", "Start my daily review", "Start my monthly review" and "Start my first review". Addresses: /daily-plan, /daily-review and /monthly-review, each with its day or month under it; the old ones (/my-day/plan, /my-day/review, /check-in) redirect with whatever follows them, their query, hash and state (`MOVED_UNDER` in `src/lib/navigation.js`). The Life balance review's own check-ins and Recovery's daily check-in keep their names. File names, database tables and saved values keep their old names. One time per tool (`TOOLS` in `src/lib/navigation.js`) feeds its tile and Home's card, so they never disagree; no time on a tool's first screen. The browser tab shows each page's full name (`tabTitle`), so a long name can show its short form on screen ("Dashboard", "Monthly review").

26b. **Performance Evidence is the Confidence Bank (Tim, 10 Nov).** Everywhere it is named: tiles, the menu, page titles, saved items, toasts, emails, PDFs and the gallery. The things inside it are **wins**: "Save a new win", "Look back at my wins", "Edit this win", "Save my win"; when it's empty its one button reads "Save my first win". No "Ask someone for a few words" button (anything already saved that way stays viewable). It has no page or address yet (it is built in a later phase, at /confidence-bank; any /performance-evidence address must redirect there). The database table keeps its name, `performance_evidence`; the stored labels change with migration 041.

26c. **Ratings are five words, never numbers (5e rule 29i; 10 Nov).** A habit rated each day and an Outcome Objective tracked by a rating both read Poor, Fair, Good, Very good, Excellent, scoring 0, 25, 50, 75 and 100 (`RATING_WORDS` and its helpers in `src/lib/performance/words.js`; docs/scoring-methods.md M1 and M9.1). Saved values keep their shape: a habit's 1 to 5, and an objective's 0 to 10, where a word saves 0, 2.5, 5, 7.5 or 10 and an earlier figure reads 0 to 2 Poor, 3 to 4 Fair, 5 to 6 Good, 7 to 8 Very good, 9 to 10 Excellent. No screen shows "/10" or "1 to 5" for these.

26d. **Way of being and Sleep quality use the same five words (Tim, 10 Nov, 23:22; docs/scoring-methods.md M12).** The Daily review asks "How calm under pressure were you today?" with Poor, Fair, Good, Very good, Excellent (never "On a scale of 1 to 10"), and the dashboard shows the average as its word ("Green weeks averaged Very good or better."). The way of being stays on its saved 1 to 10: a word saves 2, 4, 6, 8 or 10, and an older figure reads 1 to 2 Poor, 3 to 4 Fair, 5 to 6 Good, 7 to 8 Very good, 9 to 10 Excellent (`WAY_RATING_OPTIONS`, `wayRatingWord`). Sleep quality in the Daily plan (and Recovery's sleep) keeps its five saved keys, which read straight across in order: very_poor Poor, poor Fair, fair Good, good Very good, very_good Excellent (`sleepQualityWord`). No score changes and no database change. **Sleep's green starts at "Very good"** (Tim, 11 Nov, 16:35: "Good" is the middle answer and is not green). Where the dashboard and Recovery show the most common sleep answer, the label is **"Usual quality"**, never "Average quality" (it isn't an average). There is no 1 to 10 scale part any more (`ScaleChoice` and its gallery demo were removed on 11 Nov). The Monthly review's own questions keep their words.

26e. **A rated habit's week is boxes of mint, never numbers (Tim, 10 Nov, 23:22).** In the week grid (the sprint page's "This week", Home's This week line and the weekly debrief) each day is a box: a rated day is filled with one of five shades of mint, stronger for better days (`--rate-1` Poor to `--rate-5` Excellent in `tokens.css`: **in dark mode better days are brighter, in light mode darker**, kept as built (Tim, 11 Nov, 16:35), each at least 3 to 1 against the page, the surface and the boxes), with no number or word inside; a day not rated stays empty. Pointing at a box, focusing it or tapping it shows its word in the shared pop-up, a screen reader hears "Tuesday 10 Nov, Good", and tapping also opens the five words under the row to rate the day or change it (`LoggingWeekRow` kind `rating`). Checked by `tests/phase5/answers.mjs`.

27. **Times for a tool read "~5 minutes"** (Tim, 23 Oct), never "About 5 minutes" or "5 min". Ranges stay as they are, for example "1, 3 or 5 minutes".

28. **Error messages say what happened, then what to do** (Tim's rule 15), for example "That didn’t save. Check your connection and try again." "Fail" and "failed" are never used in client text. After deleting something the message reads "Deleted" (with Undo where Undo exists), apart from deleting the whole account, which says "Your account has been deleted."

29. **One add button everywhere** (Tim, 26 Oct, 16:54): every button that adds something is the shared `AddButton` (src/components/design): an outlined pill with words only and no icon (5 Nov), words that say what it adds ("Add objective", "Add agenda item", "Add a to-do", "Add another"), coral only on hover or keyboard focus, and full width on a phone. No page builds its own.

30. **Lists are rows** (Tim, 26 Oct, 16:51): when someone lists several things, each gets its own box, with "Add another" (the `AddButton`) to add a row and the 45 degree crosshair (`IconButton`) to remove one, never one big box ("one per line" or similar). Tools that still ask for several things in one box change when each tool is rebuilt.

## Rules of 5 Nov (START-HERE section 5e)

Tim's calm-pages and wording rules of 5 Nov win over anything older in this file where they differ (the eye alone with its words in a pop-up, no ⓘ beside page titles, and so on; START-HERE 5e). Each is shown with an example in the design gallery, under "Rules of 5 Nov".

- **21. One-answer questions move straight on.** The answer shows as chosen the instant it's tapped, then the next screen follows after 150ms (`ONE_TAP_MS` in GuidedTool; a step with `oneTap`). Back keeps the answer. No Next button on those screens; several-answer, typed and slider questions keep Next.
- **29o. Back lives at the top.** In every guided tool built on GuidedTool, Back is a chevron at the top left, beside the tool's name, with close at the top right. The bottom bar shows only when the screen has a button (Next, Finish, Save my change); a screen that moves straight on has none, and its Skip sits quietly under the answers. Older tools take it when they are rebuilt.
- **29t. Back chevrons line up with the content.** The chevron's visible edge sits on the content's left edge, 14px (`--back-gap`) before its label. Its 44px tap area is invisible, and on hover only the chevron turns coral, never a filled shape behind it (`.ds-guide-back`, `BackLink`).
- **29q. Times use the squiggle.** "~1 minute", never "about a minute" or "around a minute".
- **29v. Notes under buttons** (`ButtonNote`): centred on the button, 10px below it, in secondary grey, with no full stop. On a phone the button and its note run full width.
- **29x. "high-performer", "high-performers" and "high-performance"** always have the hyphen, and it never breaks across a line. Neither font has the non-breaking hyphen (U+2011), so `keepWords` keeps the word whole instead.
- **29z. One title per page:** one heading at the top, never a label title plus a headline.
- **29aa. Every answer people can tap is a full sentence in their own voice** ("The team isn't pulling in the same direction", "It's for my leadership team"), with no full stop on the button. Claude writes the new wording; the build never rewords answers on its own.
- **29y. Spelling follows the visitor:** client pages will use the spelling of the visitor's country (US time zones American, everywhere else British), from the time zone or the account's country. Not built yet: today every page is British. What decides a region today: PDF paper (`paperFor`: the country setting once there is one, then the device's region, then its time zone), units (the person's Metric or US choice), the chat app order (the time zone), and the saved time zone.
- **29am. "Edit", never "Change" (Tim, 11 Nov, 12:59).** Every link or button that opens something to alter it says "Edit": "Edit" beside an answer on a summary (a screen reader hears "Edit how it went"), "Edit my review", "Edit my answers", "Edit my target range", "Edit your areas", the eye's "Edit who can see this.". Where a row's label would read oddly after "Edit", the row gives its own words (`editName`): the sprint's "Starts" row is "Edit the start date" (Tim, 12 Nov). A question opened from a summary saves with "Save my edit". "Change it" stays where it is an answer (Keep it, Change it, Drop it), and "change" stays as a noun for a difference ("Change from 6 to 8").
- **29an. Countdowns read "3 days to go", never "3 days left"** (Tim, 11 Nov), everywhere: an objective's time (`m9TimeLeft`), Recovery's banner, the dashboard. A milestone due today reads "…is due today.", never the date and "due today" together (Tim, 12 Nov).
- **29ao. A chevron beside text is centred on that text (Tim, 11 Nov, 14:36).** Beside lowercase words its centre is on the middle of the x-height; beside capitals, the middle of the cap height; within 0.5px, open and closed: folds, drop-downs, Back, the sort link, the outside Health Check. Build it with `Chevron`'s `beside` (`'words'` or `'capitals'`; `DisclosureArrow` is `'words'` unless told): the chevron then stands on the words' own baseline inside `.ds-chevron-line`, shifted by the exact amount with **no rounding** (Tim, 12 Nov), so it holds at every text size up to twice the standard. The line must have the words' font: where the words are set in another size or font than the control, give the line the same one (as `.ds-collapse--caps`, `.ds-guide-back` and `.dash-fold-mark` do). A chevron with no words on its row (a calendar's arrows, a row's "more") leaves `beside` out. One beside a name with a line under it is centred on the two together, from the top of the name's capitals to the last line's baseline: `beside="block"` (`Collapsible`'s `twoLines`), with the place's own `--chev-block-shift` worked out from its two fonts and sizes (see `.ds-chevron--block` in `design.css`). Those are measured too and allowed 1px, because the browser places a row and each line of its words on whole screen pixels separately. The portal's own (older) Health Check route is measured as well. Measured on every page by `tests/phase5/calm-rules.mjs` (`tests/phase5/chevrons.mjs` reads the pixels).
- **29ap. No repeated second line under an answer (Tim, 11 Nov, 12:51).** An answer never has a grey second line that repeats or rewords it ("I need to perform at my best" over "Perform at my best"). A second line stays only where it adds something the answer can't say: an example ("e.g. training sessions"), a meaning, a tag. Checked on every page by calm-rules and on the lists themselves by `tests/performance/second-lines.test.mjs`.
- **29d. Small icons stand alone** (the eye, the ⓘ, the play icon), 12px (`--icon-title-gap`) from the title's last letter, level with the middle of its capitals, never on their own row: a guided step's eye is `step.eye`, a page's is PageHeader's `aside`, a panel's is the dialog's `titleAside`.
- **29h. No lonely last words:** text wraps "pretty" everywhere inside `.ui-root`, and headings, labels and buttons balance their lines (`text-wrap-style`); `keepWords` keeps a word and its number together ("week 4", "of 8").

## Action first (Tim, 26 Oct)

Every later phase follows these.

1. **The portal is extremely action focused.** Big goals are moved by immediate, small next steps, and every screen helps someone see and take the next one.
2. **Every tool ends with clarity on at least one small next step**, which links into the performance system (added to the plan, the sprint, a to-do or a habit). The Journal is the one exception for now; others only when Tim agrees.
3. **Next steps are small enough to do this week, worded as an action, and shown where people will see them again** (Home, the sprint and the weekly debrief).

## 26 Oct design rules

Tim's rules of 26 Oct (design reference v15, M4 dashboard v46). Later phases follow them. The shared parts are in `src/components/design` and shown in the design gallery.

1. **Mint means good.** One design token for good, `--good`, set to the mint token (dark #5FEFBB, light #0B7A52) and resolved per mode on the part that uses it (declared on every `.ui-root`), never once on the root. It is used for: weeks at or above the standard, rises and "+1" style figures, the live score when at or above the standard, progress bars towards a performance target, week squares, mini charts, ticked boxes, celebrations, and Saved or done. Coral is for doing and for the brand only: the one main button, hover and focus, selected states, the brand crosshair and the PDF title line. Coral is never a data colour. Grey stays for below the standard; soft coral stays for "needs you". Client-facing words call it green ("Green weeks averaged 7 hours or more."). Step bars inside guided tools (the % complete bar and the older pages' step bars) are neutral, in the text colour, not mint: guided tools are doing screens where the coral button leads.
2. **Ticked boxes fill mint**, with a black tick in dark mode and a white tick in light mode (`--on-good`).
3. **No keys on screen.** Charts label things where they are (the standard as a labelled line at its right end, "Best" and "So far" on their bars, sprint or week names along the bottom) and every mark has a pop-up. No unexplained marks.
4. **Chart pop-ups: one shared pop-up** (`ChartTips` and `tipProps`). It shows the value first and larger, then when ("Sprint 4, week 2"), then any extra fact on its own line. It stays at least 12px inside both screen edges, flips below the mark when there's no room above, wraps instead of running off the side, and is hidden with display none when closed. It opens on hover, tap and keyboard focus. A pop-up that names something says what kind of thing it is first, small, then the name ("Outcome Objective 2", then the name).
5. **Icons with words, one standard** (`IconText`, `IconTextRow`): DM Sans 14px in the secondary grey; the icon drawn in a 16px square with 1.5px lines, the same colour as its words; 6px between icon and words, measured to the icon's visible edge; 20px between items, no dots between them. The icon is centred on the middle of the capital letters, checked by measuring. A tappable one is the same at rest, turns coral on hover or keyboard focus, and has a 44px tap area. Watch for older styles with !important that block the hover colour.
6. **One chevron everywhere** (`Chevron`; `DisclosureArrow` is the chevron that turns): back, open, close and drop-down chevrons are all the corner of the brand crosshair, only rotated. No other arrow or chevron icon anywhere in the portal, My Account and its settings included, and no text arrows (← → ↗); a link's words say where it goes.
7. **The eye can be tapped to change who sees it, there and then** (`WhoSees` with `onChange`, `CoachSees`). On one thing it opens "Who can see this?" with the thing's name, then "Only you" and "You and [coach's first name]"; choosing one saves at once, shows "Saved" and updates the eye. On a page it opens "What [coach] sees" with one switch per part. Both end with "You can also change this in My Account, under What your coach sees." and use the same settings as that page. Phones get it as a bottom panel (`Sheet`). (The sharing setting arrives in Phase 1; until then the panels run on a stand-in in the design gallery.)
8. **Units beside a big figure are full words, small and grey**: "7.1 hours", "6 weeks", "8/10". Never "h" or "wks". (`Stat`'s `unit`.)
9. **Every line a person reads is a full sentence with a subject and a verb** ("You're above your standard of 85%.", never "At your standard of 85%."). One sentence per line. Labels without a full stop are the only exception.
10. **Phones show less**: on a phone, a line that repeats what a chart or figure already shows appears only on tap.
11. **Each mode uses its own colours**: every colour is checked in dark and light, on a device set to light and on one set to dark.

**Smoothness (Phase 0e).** Pages show their last data at once from the device (`useCachedLoad` in `src/lib/pageCache.js`) and refresh quietly; the turning crosshair only on a first visit. Typing is kept as a draft on the device (`useDraft` in `src/lib/drafts.js`; the shared form in FormContext) and restored quietly with "Draft restored". Saves made offline wait on the device and are sent when the connection is back (`src/lib/syncQueue.js`, `saveDecisionOrQueue`), showing "Will sync", then "Saved". When the database doesn't answer (no answer, 502, 503, 504, 52x, PGRST000 to PGRST003, a statement timeout), the queue backs off (Tim, 5 Nov): about 2, 4, 8 seconds and on, up to about 5 minutes, with ±20% randomness; new saves wait in the queue meanwhile, a hidden tab never retries, and showing the tab or the browser's online event tries once at once (`backoffDelay` in syncQueue.js, `tests/performance/sync-backoff.test.mjs`). Being offline, or a failed sign-in refresh, never signs anyone out and never clears the device; only a deliberate Sign out does (`clearAppStorage`). Errors never show raw technical text (`friendlyError`); a lasting problem adds "If it keeps happening, let your coach know." (or "use Something not working? in My Account." without a coach; `ErrorMessage keepsHappening`).

**Works on a flight (Phase 4 step 8).** A service worker keeps the app itself on the device, so the portal opens from the Home Screen with no signal. It is hand-written (`src/serviceWorker/worker.js`) and finished at build time by `vite.config.js` (`serviceWorker`), which puts the list of every file of the portal's build and of `public/` (fonts, brand images, icons) in front of it as `/sw.js`; registered only in production builds (`src/lib/serviceWorker.js`, from `startUpdateChecks`), never in development. Every portal page opened falls back to the saved `index.html`. It never keeps database or sign-in answers (data offline comes from `pageCache` only), and it leaves the outside Health Check (`health-check.html`, `/health-check/*` and everything its page asks for), `/api/`, the design reference, the sign-in email links (`/auth/callback`, `/auth/reset-password`, `/email-confirmation`, `/check-email-confirmation`), `/version.json` and `/sw.js` to the network. The Chinese font parts PDFs use are kept only once used. After sign-in and once per online visit, `src/lib/warmDevice.js` quietly reads the key pages' data with their own loaders into their device copies (Home's day, the plan, the sprint, Daily plan, Daily review, this week's debrief, My Plans, My Reviews; one at a time, nothing under 10 minutes old, never while saves wait). A page never opened, with no copy yet, says so with `LoadError` ("This page needs a connection the first time it opens on this device, so try again when you’re back online."). What truly needs a connection says so in one shape, `needsConnection` in `friendlyError.js` ("Signing in needs a connection, so try again when you’re back online."; pass the words as `friendlyError`'s third argument). The queue waits for a refreshed sign-in before sending and tries again on its own after a refused refresh (10, 20, 40, then every 60 seconds). A new version installs quietly and waits; it takes over on the next full open, or (`appUpdate.js`) when the page is hidden and nobody is typing, no dialog is open and nothing is being sent; drafts and waiting saves are on the device, so they survive it. Manifest `start_url` and `scope` stay "/". Check it with `tests/phase4/offline.mjs` (two production builds on the test project, served like Vercel by `tests/phase4/serve-build.mjs`).

**Text size follows the device** (Claude's call, 26 Oct): every text size is a multiple of `--u` (`calc(17 * var(--u))`), never a fixed px size, so text grows with the browser's text-size setting and, on iPhone and iPad, the Text Size setting (the one allowed global rule on html, in `src/index.css`), up to twice the standard size. Big display text (Nekst titles, big numbers, anything 20px and up) uses `--u-big` and grows by at most a quarter, so spaced capitals never split mid-word; the phone tab bar's labels stay 10px. Spacing, icons and tap areas stay fixed; layouts must wrap rather than break at the largest size (`tests/phase0e/app.mjs` checks every page at twice the standard size).

## PDF rules

Tim's PDF rules (26 Oct). Every PDF follows all 33. They are built into the shared template in `src/lib/pdf` (pdfkit, with its own page layout step), so no tool draws its own PDF: a tool describes its document (see the top of `src/lib/pdf/render.js`) and the template does the rest. The one Download PDF button is `PdfDownload` (src/components/design).

**Versions and paper**

1. **Light and Dark for tools** (Tim, 08:59): every tool that makes a PDF offers Light (for printing) and Dark (for sharing on a phone). Same layout, content and page breaks; only the colours and the logo file change. Coaching agreements, sponsor agreements, quotes and the supplier pack are light only, because they are printed, signed and filed.
2. **One button, then a choice:** one "Download PDF" button, which then offers "Light, for printing" and "Dark, for sharing".
3. **Paper matches the client's country** (Tim, 09:20): US Letter for the US and Canada, A4 for everyone else. Margins about 19mm on every side, with the footer inside them. (One shared helper, `paperFor` in `src/lib/pdf/paper.js`: the person's country setting once My Account has one, then the device's region, then its time zone.)
4. **No cover page** (Tim, 09:20): page 1 opens with the title block, then the content straight away.

**On every page**

5. **Title block (page 1):** the title in Nekst capitals, one or two lines; the 56px coral line; one grey line of details (organisation or person, date, type), each readable on its own ("Board or owner meeting", not "Board or owner").
5a. **Titles people type are limited to 80 characters, so a PDF title always fits two lines.** (The meeting plan's title, with the note "Titles can be up to 80 characters."; `src/lib/titleLimit.js`. The title block still shrinks to fit for older saved titles.)
6. **Updated, not version numbers** (Tim, 09:33): a tool PDF downloaded again after changes shows "Updated Mon 26 Oct 2026, 09:30" in its details line. Signed documents keep their reference and version number.
7. **Running header from page 2** (Tim, 09:20): small and grey: the document name on the left, the organisation or person on the right.
8. **Footer:** the real logo mark, the portal address as a working link, "Confidential: for [organisation or person]" when it holds a client's or team's own answers (Tim, 09:20), and "Page 2 of 4". A one-page PDF has no page number.
9. **The bold crosshair in the footer** (Tim, 26 Oct, 16:13, replacing the logomark PNGs): every PDF footer carries the bold crosshair, the 2 times line version used on the website's menu button and Home Screen icon, black on light pages and white on dark pages, drawn from the same shared bold crosshair (`src/lib/boldCrosshair.js`), never redrawn or recoloured. Logo files are still never redrawn or recoloured in mock-ups.
10. **No "Prepared by" panel** (Tim, 09:04). Who made it only shows where a signature is needed.

**What goes in**

11. **Blank answers are left out** (Tim, 09:33): the PDF shows only what was filled in, so it never looks half done. A section with nothing in it is left out with its heading.
12. **Summary first:** page 1 starts with an At a glance panel of two to four items, each laid out the same way.
13. **Contents list over 6 pages** (Tim, 09:33): a short list of sections with page numbers, under the At a glance panel.
14. **No link back to the portal** (Tim, 09:33): the PDF stands on its own; the portal address in the footer is enough.

**Type and colour**

15. **Fonts:** Nekst for the title, section headings (capitals, slightly letter-spaced) and big numbers (Nekst Light at 22pt and above); DM Sans for everything else. Body text 10pt with generous line spacing, lines no longer than about 62 characters, nothing smaller than 8.5pt. Words are never hyphenated across lines.
16. **Light colours:** white page, near-black text (#111110), grey for details (#636362), pale grey lines (#DCDCD9), light grey panels (#F4F4F3). **Dark colours:** black page, white text, grey details (#A3A3A0), dark lines (#262626), dark panels (#111110) with a thin edge so they hold on a dim phone. (These live in `src/lib/pdf/theme.js`, the one place a PDF colour is written.)
17. **Green means good** (Tim, 26 Oct; was coral): what is at or above the standard is green, deep green #0B7A52 on light pages and mint #5FEFBB on dark pages (`good` in `src/lib/pdf/theme.js`); below the standard is grey. Coral is only the title line. Never coral or green text. Keys call it green ("Green: important and moving well").
18. **Colour is never the only signal:** every bar has its value beside it and every chart has a key, so a black and white print still reads.
19. **The writing rules apply** (as in this file): labels before numbers, UK spelling, dates like "Mon 26 Oct 2026", 24-hour times, no dashes, "Dr. Tim Dutton" and "The Clarity Project LTD" exactly so.

**Parts**

20. **Shared parts only:** title block, running header, footer, At a glance panel, contents list, section heading, text, table, bar chart, dot chart, list and signature block. No tool draws its own. (The dot chart, as in the Health Check: one dot per item on labelled axes, divided by lines at the middle of each axis, with no quadrant names, as on the results page; green filled dots only for the good area, grey outlines for the rest; each dot numbered, with names beside the dots only when none would touch a label, dot or line, otherwise a numbered list beside or under the chart, or after the chart's key and sentence when the names are too many to fit with it; a key and a sentence after it. Behind the dots, the same pale areas as the results page (Tim, 26 Oct, 16:14): a field of small dots, pale green where projects are important and moving well and the pale line colour everywhere else, never coral, pale enough for a black and white print; each project dot has the same soft halo as on the results page.)
21. **Charts:** drawn to scale from zero, with the standard marked, values at the end of each bar, a key underneath, and a sentence after it saying what it means.
22. **Tables:** thin lines between rows only, no boxes or fills, a grey header row, numbers lined up on the right.

**Page breaks: nothing chopped** (Tim, 09:21 and 09:30)

23. **Kept together, always:** a heading with at least three lines of what follows; a panel; a chart with its key and its sentence; a table row; a list item; a question with its answer's first three lines; the signature block.
24. **Short sections move whole:** a section shorter than about a third of a page that won't fit in the space left moves to the next page whole.
25. **Long sections split only at natural points:** between paragraphs, table rows or list items, never inside one.
26. **Long answers split with three lines each side:** a client's long answer can carry on to the next page only between paragraphs, with at least three lines left behind and three carried over.
27. **Tables that carry on:** the next page repeats the heading with ", continued" ("Projects ranked by alignment, continued") and the column names.
28. **No stray lines:** never one or two lines alone at the top or bottom of a page, and a last page never holds just a line or two. Space at the end of a page is fine; content is never stretched to fill it.
29. **Light and dark break in the same places,** and A4 and US Letter are each checked.

**The file and checks**

30. **Real and readable:** fonts embedded, text selectable and searchable, links that work; tagged for screen readers with the right reading order, a text description for every chart, the language set to English (UK), and a contents list (bookmarks) in every PDF (rule 33). Bookmarks, the file's title and copied heading text are whole words in sentence case, never split by letter-spacing. (See rule 33 for how spaced capitals are drawn; `tests/pdf/make-pdfs.mjs` checks every bookmark, title and heading.)
30a. **No "…" anywhere in a PDF** (Tim, 26 Oct, 16:12): nothing is ever cut off. A long name wraps onto more lines instead: the footer's "Confidential: for [organisation]" line wraps upwards inside the footer and the running header's names wrap downwards, both bands growing to hold them inside the margins, with nothing overlapping; chart labels wrap or, for a single axis word, get smaller down to 8.5pt. `tests/pdf/make-pdfs.mjs` fails any PDF whose text holds "…", and checks the footer shows a long organisation name in full.
31. **Named in words and small:** for example "Health Check, Northfield Logistics, 26 Oct 2026.pdf", with "(dark)" at the end of the dark one; the file's properties carry the same title and "Dr. Tim Dutton, The Clarity Project LTD" as author; a typical PDF is under 1 MB.
32. **Stress-test pack before Tim sees it** (Tim, 09:30): every new or changed PDF is made with very short, very long and awkward content (long names, long answers, long tables, emoji, blank answers), in light and dark, on A4 and US Letter; every page break is checked against rules 23 to 29; Tim gets the pack to look through. Plus: open on a Mac and an iPhone, print a light page in black and white, and send the dark one through a chat app. Invented names only.
33. **Whole words for every reader** (Tim, 28 Oct): spaced capitals (titles, section headings, panel headings) must copy, search, read aloud and appear in the contents list as whole words, in every PDF viewer. One run of text with the PDF's letter-spacing was not enough: viewers that guess word gaps from where letters sit split it ("O BJECTIVE", "AGAI N S T"), because the letter-spacing is close to the gap they read as a space. So the capitals are drawn with the font's own letter shapes, with the same spacing, and the written words (sentence case) lie over them as one invisible run of text without letter-spacing, stretched to the same width; their tags keep the written words too (`drawSpaced` in `src/lib/pdf/text.js`). Every PDF has a contents list (bookmarks): each section once, by its name as written, where it starts, never again for a table carried on, and the document's title when it has no sections; and a document title. The PDF pack reads every PDF a second way, with pdfminer (`pip3 install pdfminer.six`), which guesses gaps like those viewers, and fails if a heading's words come out split, a bookmark is missing, repeated or for a carried-on table.

**How to check a PDF** (for rules 23 to 32): `node tests/pdf/make-pdfs.mjs <folder>` makes every PDF and the stress cases in light and dark, on A4 and US Letter, checks every page break and writes `break-report.txt`; add new documents there. Check the tags with veraPDF (`brew install verapdf`, then `verapdf --flavour ua1 --format text <folder>/*.pdf`; every file should say PASS). Fonts for PDFs are TTF or WOFF, never WOFF2: pdfkit cannot build in a WOFF2 font's letter shapes, so its text comes out blank.

## List row guidelines

These are guidelines, to apply by default to every list built from the shared row (`src/components/ui/HistoryList.jsx`: My Plans, My Reviews, My Journals). Where a mock-up shows a detail, the mock-up wins. They are about layout only, not saving or navigation.

Every row is built the same way (Phase 4 step 4, writing rule 60, Tim 23 Oct):

1. **Line 1:** the name in words, left-aligned, wrapping only between words, and the date on the right ("Today", "Yesterday", "Mon 19 Oct"; `formatSavedDate`). Never a date as the name: day plans read "Today’s plan", "Yesterday’s plan", then "Day plan" (reviews the same), debriefs "Sprint 4, week 3", a journal "Journal entry", never its first words. The names live in one place, `src/lib/savedItems.js`.
2. **Line 2:** the tool, then the status tag (Draft, Reviewed) beside it, then the version chip (for example Version 1.2) beside the tag, all on one left-aligned line that wraps cleanly.
3. **Line 3:** any count (for example 196 words), left-aligned under line 2.
4. **Actions:** on a phone, one right-aligned row beneath with a small, even gap above it; on a laptop, at the right of the row in a fixed width so the dates line up. The text action (Continue, Review, View review) is the same size and weight as the tool, not larger; the pencil and bin icons are the same size and distance apart in every row. A row with nothing to do has no action row.
5. Every element starts at the same left edge in every row: no indents, no centring.
6. **Filters:** filters sit behind one Filter button (the shared `FilterButton`), with one line saying what is applied and Clear (Tim, 22 Oct). My Plans and My Reviews filter by area (Personal, Interpersonal, Strategic), then by tool (Strategic: by team); `?area=` and `&tool=` or `&team=` open them filtered.

My Plans and My Reviews are one list each across the three areas, holding every saved item, old and new (`src/lib/savedItemsApi.js`). The old My decisions list (`/decision-history`) redirects to My Plans. Plan, Review and Interpersonal hubs end with their last three saved items (`HubRecent`), never journal entries; Strategic's Health Check card lists the saved teams (`HubTeams`).

## Time zones (time-zone-safeguards.md; Tim, 10 Nov)

**One small time library, `src/lib/time`, does all of this. Don't add a second way.** (The device's own clock on screen stays with rule 19's `dateFormatter.js`: inside the portal the screen already shows the person's own time.)

1. **Every call is anchored to the client's time zone** (group calls: the booker's, or the organisation's main office). The coach's time is worked out from it each time it is shown.
2. **A time is saved as a UTC instant together with the place name of its anchor zone** (`savedTime`: `{ at: '…Z', zone: 'America/New_York' }`). Offsets ("-05:00") and letters ("EST") are never saved or accepted as a zone (`isZone`, `mustBeZone`). A day or a time that doesn't exist (30 Feb, 24:00) is refused with a clear error, never rolled on to another day, and only a real place-name zone from the device is ever saved on a person's row.
3. **A repeating series is saved as a rule in its anchor zone** ("every other Monday, 07:30 New York time"; `seriesRule`, `seriesCalls`), never as a list of instants, so it never drifts when either country's clocks change.
4. **Times are written with a city or country name, never letters** (`zoneLabel`: "New York time", "UK time"), and never rounded to the hour (India, Newfoundland, Nepal). The names come from fixed lists in `zones.js` (11 Nov): a zone named after a state's sub-area, a small place or a region reads as the main city that keeps exactly the same clock all year (America/Indiana/Knox is "Chicago time", never "Knox time"), and an island or a station whose bare name reads oddly gets its full name ("Christmas Island time"). A test reads every zone the device knows: no underscores, no sub-areas, and every mapped zone keeps its main city's clock for two years.
5. **12-hour for readers in the US and Canada, 24-hour for everyone else** (`usesTwelveHour`, `clockWords`). The coach sees both: "12:30 UK time · 7:30 am New York time" (`bothWords`).
6. **Both days are shown when they differ**: "Mon 16 Nov, 08:00 Sydney time (Sun 15 Nov, 21:00 UK time)". **Dates are written out in words**, never numbers.
7. **Anything the client sees runs on the client's own midnight**: the Daily plan, the Daily review, streaks, sprint days, due dates and "today's to-dos" (`localToday`, the device's own day; `dayIn(zone)` for a named zone). The server's own "today" (`person_today()`) uses the person's saved zone, which the app sends before it asks the server to close a week.
8. **Booking** (helpers for Phase 6a, `booking.js`): starts only on a quarter hour in the client's time; never within 15 minutes of another booking; only calls that start at 07:00 or later and **end by 20:00** in the booker's own time (the client's, when the client books), unless they choose "Show early and late times" (11 Nov: a 60-minute call's last start is 19:00, a 30-minute call's 19:30).
9. **Clock changes** (`clockChanges`, `callsThatMove`, `clockChangeLine`): the changes coming up in the next 7 days for any zone, and which calls move for the coach, for the quiet line in the coach portal. **Public holidays** in the client's country (`publicHolidayOn`, from the pinned `date-holidays` package, loaded only when a booking is checked): "That’s Thanksgiving in the US."; UK bank holidays need the nation and some Canadian ones the province, and the day is read on the country's own calendar whatever zone the device or server is in. A holiday moved off a weekend reads "(observed)", never "(substitute day)" (`holidayName`). The holiday data's licence (CC-BY-SA-4.0) is credited in `docs/credits.md`, and the same line (`HOLIDAY_DATA_CREDIT`) goes in the help page's footer when the help page is built.
