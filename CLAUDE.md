## Pushing and previews (Tim, 23 Nov 2026)

Push `feature/performance-overhaul` after every commit, never `main`. **A preview builds only when the latest commit message contains `[preview]`** (`ignoreCommand` in `vercel.json`, `scripts/vercel-ignore.sh`), because Vercel's free team has 10 GB of deployment storage; production builds from `main` always. **Make exactly one commit marked `[preview]` at the end of each run**, after its report is written and every test has run, so Tim gets one preview per run. Helpers and testers never write `[preview]`, and no other commit message may contain it, not even when describing this rule (the commit that added the rule named it and so built a preview).

5. **Icons with words, one standard** (`IconText`, `IconTextRow`): DM Sans 14px in the secondary grey; the icon drawn in a 16px square with 1.5px lines, the same colour as its words; 6px between icon and words, measured to the icon's visible edge; 20px between items, no dots between them. The icon is centred on the middle of the capital letters, checked by measuring. A tappable one is the same at rest, turns coral on hover or keyboard focus, and has a 44px tap area. Watch for older styles with !important that block the hover colour.
6. **One chevron everywhere** (`Chevron`; `DisclosureArrow` is the chevron that turns): back, open, close and drop-down chevrons are all the corner of the brand crosshair, only rotated. No other arrow or chevron icon anywhere in the portal, My Account and its settings included, and no text arrows (← → ↗); a link's words say where it goes.
7. **The eye can be tapped to change who sees it, there and then** (`WhoSees` with `onChange`, `CoachSees`). On one thing it opens "Who can see this?" with the thing's name, then "Only you" and "You and [coach's first name]"; choosing one saves at once, shows "Saved" and updates the eye. On a page it opens "What [coach] sees" with one switch per part. Both end with "You can also change this in My Account, under What your coach sees." and use the same settings as that page. **"Who can see this?" is the side panel from the right, as M9, M10 and M7 draw it** (Phase 8a follow-up, 17 Nov; `SlidePanel` with `ds-who-panel`: 460px, `--panel-w-calm`, the title a question in DM Sans at 20px, the two choices as M9's answer rows with the tick, full screen on a phone), one shared part for every eye, a guest's included; "What [coach] sees" stays the small panel (`Sheet`) M4 draws. (The sharing setting arrives in Phase 1; until then the panels run on a stand-in in the design gallery.)
8. **Units beside a big figure are full words, small and grey**: "7.1 hours", "6 weeks", "8/10". Never "h" or "wks". (`Stat`'s `unit`.)
9. **Every line a person reads is a full sentence with a subject and a verb** ("You're above your standard of 85%.", never "At your standard of 85%."). One sentence per line. Labels without a full stop are the only exception.
10. **Phones show less**: on a phone, a line that repeats what a chart or figure already shows appears only on tap.
11. **Each mode uses its own colours**: every colour is checked in dark and light, on a device set to light and on one set to dark.

**Smoothness (Phase 0e).** Pages show their last data at once from the device (`useCachedLoad` in `src/lib/pageCache.js`) and refresh quietly; the turning crosshair only on a first visit. Typing is kept as a draft on the device (`useDraft` in `src/lib/drafts.js`; the shared form in FormContext) and restored quietly with "Draft restored". **A slide-in panel with boxes to type in keeps its draft** (tidy pass, 22 Nov): give the shared `SlidePanel` a `draftKey` naming what is added or edited (`lead-note:<id>`); closed with its cross, Escape, a tap outside, a swipe, the phone's Back or Cancel (`useDismissPanel`) the typing stays and comes back next time it opens, and the panel's own button closing it (a save) lets it go (`src/lib/panelDraft.js`, `tests/phase0e/panel-drafts.mjs`). Saves made offline wait on the device and are sent when the connection is back (`src/lib/syncQueue.js`, `saveDecisionOrQueue`), showing "Will sync", then "Saved". **Finish: newest choice wins** (Tim, 17 Nov): with the same item open in two tabs, Finish in either tab finishes it with that tab's answers, even when the other tab saved since; it is never refused as another window's and never stays a draft (the queue's `newestWins`, used by the Monthly review, Daily plan, Daily review and weekly debrief; checked by `tests/phase5/breakit.mjs`). A stale tab's later save is still checked, so it is refused and shows the finished item. When the database doesn't answer (no answer, 502, 503, 504, 52x, PGRST000 to PGRST003, a statement timeout), the queue backs off (Tim, 5 Nov): about 2, 4, 8 seconds and on, up to about 5 minutes, with ±20% randomness; new saves wait in the queue meanwhile, a hidden tab never retries, and showing the tab or the browser's online event tries once at once (`backoffDelay` in syncQueue.js, `tests/performance/sync-backoff.test.mjs`). Being offline, or a failed sign-in refresh, never signs anyone out and never clears the device; only a deliberate Sign out does (`clearAppStorage`). **Sign out clears every saved copy of the person's on the device** (tidy follow-up 3), own questions, the day's plan order, peak moments and travel plans included (`PERSONAL_KINDS`), after sending across what still waits in them (`src/lib/signOutCopies.js`; "Some changes haven’t synced yet." when something can't go). A new storage key is either cleared there or listed with its reason in `tests/performance/sign-out-clears.test.mjs`, which reads every key in src. Errors never show raw technical text (`friendlyError`); a lasting problem adds "If it keeps happening, let your coach know." (or "use Something not working? in My Account." without a coach; `ErrorMessage keepsHappening`).

**Works on a flight (Phase 4 step 8).** A service worker keeps the app itself on the device, so the portal opens from the Home Screen with no signal. It is hand-written (`src/serviceWorker/worker.js`) and finished at build time by `vite.config.js` (`serviceWorker`), which puts the list of every file of the portal's build and of `public/` (fonts, brand images, icons) in front of it as `/sw.js`; registered only in production builds (`src/lib/serviceWorker.js`, from `startUpdateChecks`), never in development. Every portal page opened falls back to the saved `index.html`. It never keeps the Quick guide's videos and captions (left to the network: about 11 MB, and a browser asks for a video in pieces), nor database or sign-in answers (data offline comes from `pageCache` only), and it leaves the outside Health Check (`health-check.html`, `/health-check/*` and everything its page asks for), `/api/`, the design reference, the addresses a sign-in email link or Google comes back to (`/auth/google`, `/auth/confirmed`, `/auth/invite`, `/auth/reset-password`, and the older `/auth/callback`, `/auth/mission`, `/email-confirmation`, `/check-email-confirmation`), `/version.json` and `/sw.js` to the network. The Chinese font parts PDFs use are kept only once used. After sign-in and once per online visit, `src/lib/warmDevice.js` quietly reads the key pages' data with their own loaders into their device copies (Home's day, the plan, the sprint, Daily plan, Daily review, this week's debrief, My Plans, My Reviews; one at a time, nothing under 10 minutes old, never while saves wait). A page never opened, with no copy yet, says so with `LoadError` ("This page needs a connection the first time it opens on this device, so try again when you’re back online."). What truly needs a connection says so in one shape, `needsConnection` in `friendlyError.js` ("Signing in needs a connection, so try again when you’re back online."; pass the words as `friendlyError`'s third argument). The queue waits for a refreshed sign-in before sending and tries again on its own after a refused refresh (10, 20, 40, then every 60 seconds). A new version installs quietly and waits; it takes over on the next full open, or (`appUpdate.js`) when the page is hidden and nobody is typing, no dialog is open and nothing is being sent; drafts and waiting saves are on the device, so they survive it. Manifest `start_url` and `scope` stay "/". Check it with `tests/phase4/offline.mjs` (two production builds on the test project, served like Vercel by `tests/phase4/serve-build.mjs`).

**Text size follows the device** (Claude's call, 26 Oct): every text size is a multiple of `--u` (`calc(17 * var(--u))`), never a fixed px size, so text grows with the browser's text-size setting and, on iPhone and iPad, the Text Size setting (the one allowed global rule on html, in `src/index.css`), up to twice the standard size. Big display text (Nekst titles, big numbers, anything 20px and up) uses `--u-big` and grows by at most a quarter, so spaced capitals never split mid-word; the phone tab bar's labels stay 10px. Spacing, icons and tap areas stay fixed; layouts must wrap rather than break at the largest size (`tests/phase0e/app.mjs` checks every page at twice the standard size).

## Browser suites run on the local stand-in (Tim, 25 Nov 2026)

The hosted test project (clarity-portal-test) went over Supabase's free quota (egress 7.7 of 5 GB, logs 6.9 of 1 GB in one cycle), almost all from suites and testers. **Every browser suite, tester and load test runs on the local stand-in by default** (`tests/scale/stack.mjs`; the four standing accounts from `tests/scale/seed/05-standing-accounts.sql`; the app built with `VITE_SUPABASE_URL=http://127.0.0.1:54400`). Suites get their accounts and database host only from `tests/target.mjs`; `HOSTED=1` points one at the hosted project, only for **the end-of-run smoke check** (`HOSTED=1 node --import ./tests/scores/register.mjs tests/smoke/hosted.mjs`: sign-in, one page per area, one PDF, nothing written) or a suite that truly needs real Supabase (`tests/coach/real-sign-in.mjs`), once, never in a loop. **Large seeds, scale runs and load tests never touch a hosted target**: `tests/hosted-guard.mjs` stops them (tests/scale's tools, and `scripts/dry-run-on-test.sh` refuses `tests/scale/` SQL), and `tests/performance/hosted-guard.test.mjs` fails a suite that reads the accounts file or names the hosted project itself. Put this in every tester's and helper's brief.
