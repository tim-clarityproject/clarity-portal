## Pushing and previews (Tim, 23 Nov 2026)

Push `feature/performance-overhaul` after every commit, never `main`. **A preview builds only when the latest commit message contains `[preview]`** (`ignoreCommand` in `vercel.json`, `scripts/vercel-ignore.sh`), because Vercel's free team has 10 GB of deployment storage; production builds from `main` always. **Make exactly one commit marked `[preview]` at the end of each run**, after its report is written and every test has run, so Tim gets one preview per run. Helpers and testers never write `[preview]`, and no other commit message may contain it, not even when describing this rule (the commit that added the rule named it and so built a preview).

## Design rules (Clarity Portal)

1. **Dark and light modes both exist**, chosen as Light, Dark or Match my device (the starting choice), in My Account under Appearance. **Every page follows the choice** (Tim, 10 Nov), the older pages not yet rebuilt included: nothing in the portal is held in dark any more (`src/lib/colourMode/boot.js`). The outside Health Check (`/health-check`) has no Appearance setting, so it follows the person's device, light or dark (Tim, 10 Nov, 23:22): its own page (`health-check.html`), built from the portal's shared parts since 14 Nov and set by the same boot file, and the portal's own route through the same boot file, whatever was chosen for the portal on that device. It is checked in both modes by `tests/phase5/calm-rules.mjs`. Every colour comes from a token with a dark and a light value, so check any page you touch in both. **DM Sans is the font Google Fonts serves, with its optical sizes** (Phase 8a follow-up, 17 Nov; `src/styles/dm-sans.css` from `@fontsource-variable/dm-sans` 5.3.0, pinned, the portal's own files), so built pages match the mock-ups' widths; every comparison hands the mock-up the same file. PDFs keep the static WOFF files (`@fontsource/dm-sans`).

2. **Never hard-code a colour, font, font size, spacing or timing.** All design values must come from `src/styles/tokens.css` (CSS variables starting with `--`) and all component classes from `src/styles/components.css`. This includes button styles, borders, text sizes, padding, gaps, animation duration, and motion curves.

3. **No gradients anywhere in the interface.** Buttons, borders, backgrounds and other elements use flat single colours only. The only gradient allowed anywhere is inside the supplied logo image files (which are never modified).

4. **Nekst capitals only for page titles and section names.** Always uppercase with wide letter spacing, never below 10px, never in paragraphs. **Buttons are never Nekst capitals** (Tim, 18 Nov: rule 4a). Small labels, question headings and everything else are DM Sans in sentence case (paragraphs, descriptions, typed answers, drop-down options and inputs; inputs 16px minimum). No overlines.

4a. **Buttons and controls: Quiet precision (Tim, 18 Nov 2026; follow-up 1 of 15:57 wins where the two differ), every button in the portal, the client portal and the coach portal alike** (`src/styles/buttons.css`, the one place their look is written). **Buttons, segmented switches, form choices (Zoom or In person; 30, 60 or 90 minutes; light or dark) and filter buttons are one family**: DM Sans 16px, sentence case, weight 400 (the coral main button 450; DM Sans loads its full variable range), letter-spacing -0.005em; 40px high on a laptop and 44px on a phone (`--btn-h`, `--btn-h-phone`); 16px in at each side; 8px corners. Chips have 6px corners. **Button words grow with the device's text size up to twice** (WCAG resize text): a button then grows taller and wraps onto centred lines, never cutting a word off or running out of its card. **The tap area stays 44px**: an invisible ::after, 44px high and as wide as the button, centred on it (calm-rules counts it, and still fails anything under 44px). The main button (still one per screen): flat coral with black text and a faint top highlight (`--btn-highlight`), each mode's coral hover. Every other button: no fill, a 1px hairline drawn as an inset shadow in the outline grey (`--btn-line`, #3C3C3B dark, #B9B7B1 light), the words in the secondary grey; on hover and keyboard focus the hairline and the words turn the text colour. The hairline is the agreed exception to rule 8a: the words identify the button. Pressing scales to 0.97 for 150ms; Reduce motion turns it off. **A segmented switch** sits on a quiet grey surface (`--seg-track`) with 8px corners; the chosen option is a soft raised tile with 6px corners (`--seg-tile`: the card colour in light, a step up in dark, where the card colour would vanish on the surface) with a very faint shadow, sliding to a new choice in 220ms (`--dur-glide`, the --ease curve; Reduce motion: it moves without sliding); the others are plain text (`InlineChoice`'s `.ds-seg-tile`). **One switch everywhere**: a 40 by 24px track with a round knob, off the quiet grey track, on the mint track (never coral) with the knob at the right; the whole row holding it is its tap area (`.ds-switch-tap`). **Text inputs and drop-downs are one height**: 56px on a laptop, 52px on a phone (`--field-h`), 14px corners; boxes, choices and cards side by side in one row are the same height. **No Nekst inside any control** (buttons, chips, choices, steppers, calendar day numbers, the My Mission pill's label, Back links, the saved-item rows' tags): Nekst stays for page titles, section headings (a fold's heading row included) and big figures. **The coral focus ring shows only for keyboard focus** (`:focus-visible`); a text field takes the stronger grey edge on focus and an inline edit opens with the cursor at the end. **Back's chevron is 10px from its words** (`--back-gap`) on every page. **Folds open from the whole heading row.** **No coloured side lines** on any card, row or calendar event, with no exception (Tim, 18 Nov, 12:55). **No line directly under a section heading when rows with their own dividers follow** (`ResultsSection`'s `rows`). **The highest priority is a soft coral tick box** (a 2px edge in `--coral-soft-text`, #FFBD9E dark, #A84E28 light; the title unchanged; `TodoRow`'s `important`); **where there is no tick box, a small soft coral box before the title** (Tim, 18 Nov, 12:55, which replaced his 12:30 thin line; ~/Downloads/buttons-followup-1.md point 5), drawn exactly as that tick box's square: 18px (`--checkbox-box`), a 2px `--coral-soft-text` edge, the tick box's corners, no fill (`.ds-important-mark` in a `.ds-mark-row`, the title `--important-mark-gap` after it), the title unchanged and in line with the titles around it, which keep the box's space (`.ds-mark-space`): the Daily plan's summary, the calendar's to-dos to place and a coach to-do while choosing several on a phone, where the tick box steps aside. Nothing else marks it (no side line, no bold, no coral word). **Back's chevron 10px from its words and Important's two forms are lasting checks** (`backGaps`, `importantMarks` in `tests/buttons/buttons-check.mjs`, run by calm-rules, the coach UI tester and controls-sweep; fixtures in `tests/buttons/back-and-important.test.mjs`). **Offer rows have no icon** (rule 8b). The floating Breathe pill keeps its size (it holds the orb) with its word in DM Sans. **The gliding switch is the one toggle (Tim, 22 Nov; design reference 22 Nov):** every choice between views or answers is the soft grey track with 8px corners and the chosen option raised; the round pill toggle (`.seg`) is retired, underlined tabs stay for moving between parts of a page, and `controlsFamily` in `tests/buttons/buttons-check.mjs` checks every page (calm-rules, the coach UI tester and `tests/buttons/controls-sweep.mjs`), failing a round pill toggle, Nekst inside a control, a control of the wrong height or corners, a switch that isn't 40 by 24 and mint when on, or a second coral button (docs/rules/controls.md). Checked on every page by calm-rules (`tests/buttons/buttons-check.mjs`: `buttonProblems`, `sideBySide`, `nekstInControls`, `sideLines`, `doubledRules`, `chevronOnlyFolds`, `backGaps`, `importantMarks`), and in the mock-up comparisons, which expect each button in the mock-up's slot in the new style (`newButton`, `shiftForNewButtons` in `tests/phase8/compare-lib.mjs`; `tests/buttons/expected.mjs`).

5. **Coral (#FF6F5C) is for actions and the active tab. Mint (#5FEFBB, deep green #0B7A52 in light) means good: success, saved, done, and data at or above the standard (see the 26 Oct design rules).** Buttons use black text (#000000) on flat coral (rule 4a). The older button classes are `.ui-btn-primary` (filled coral) and `.ui-btn-ghost` (outlined), drawn in the same new look. Use var(--coral), var(--good) and var(--mint-text) only.

6. **The standard crosshair (Crosshair, cropped from the logo) is used only, turned 45 degrees, for close.** Never as a plus: the add row draws its own small coral cross (rule 29), and steppers use the plain minus and plus (`StepIcon`: 16px, 1.5px lines, round ends; 5e rules 29e and 29g, swept on 10 Nov). Anything that opens a list or a folded section (drop-downs, folds, expandable rows, the date box) uses DisclosureArrow, one half of the same crosshair crop, pointing down and turning to point up while open; back and next use the same chevron turned (Chevron, 26 Oct design rule 6). Two exceptions: the turning crosshair of the loading spinner, and the breathing orb's 8px crosshair. The bold crosshair (BoldCrosshair) is used only for the Home Screen icon, the favicon, the desktop menu button, the phone bar's centre button and every PDF footer (Tim, 26 Oct, 16:13), always drawn from its one shared drawing (`src/lib/boldCrosshair.js`). The crosshair is never smaller than 14px apart from the orb, and never redrawn.

7. **Page titles use the shared PageHeader** (src/components/design): the title, a quiet meta line, and any explanation behind the small "i". No overline and no intro line under the title. Each page's explanation lives in src/content/pageText.js and shows as one line behind the "i".

8. **Use the word Mission everywhere, never Purpose, always with a capital M** (Tim, 10 Nov, 23:22): "Edit my Mission", "Save my Mission", "What’s your Mission?", "Your Mission isn’t written yet.", matching the My Mission pill. Never "my mission" or "your mission" in anything people read (code names, addresses and database values keep theirs; the legal text's "mission statement" waits for the lawyer's draft).

8a. **Edges people rely on reach 3 to 1** (buttons excepted since 18 Nov: Tim's hairline in the outline grey, rule 4a; the words carry the button) (design reference: "Box edges"; Tim, 11 Nov). An answer row at rest, a row that opens levels, a dashed empty day box and the outside Health Check's outlined buttons and answer rows all take the edges colour (`--field-line`: #636362 in dark, #858480 in light; the outside Health Check uses the same token since 14 Nov), never the fainter line colours. Hover is a step stronger (`--text-2`), chosen is the text colour. Measured by `tests/phase5/answers2.mjs`. **Every outlined edge does** (Tim, 12 Nov): the Breathe and chat buttons, the segmented control's outer edge, chips (the journal's prompts), a guided tool's current-step pill, the sign-in screens' fields and example pills (`.auth-input`, `.auth-example-pill`), and the older pages' text boxes and steppers (`.plan-input`; `.tsa-stepper-btn` and the stepper's own box, with the button's hover a step stronger, `--text-2`) all take `--field-line`. `--line`, `--btn-line` and `--outline` are for lines between rows and for chart marks, never for the outline of something to tap or type into. Checked on every page by calm-rules (`tests/phase5/edges.mjs` reads every outline with a border all the way round, a field's edge drawn on the box that holds it included; not one drawn with `outline` or a shadow, and not hover or focus) and by `tests/phase5/answers3.mjs`.

8b. **Windows high-contrast mode (forced colours; Tim, 12 Nov).** A masked icon (the crosshair, the chevron, a tick) is a flat colour seen through a mask, and that mode replaces the colour, so each one sets `forced-color-adjust: none` and the system's text colour (`CanvasText`) under `@media (forced-colors: active)`: `design.css` and `components.css` (the Health Check's own marks are in `src/pages/healthCheck/healthCheck.css`). A new masked icon needs the same. A tick drawn as a line on a filled shape (a ticked box, a chosen pick-several answer, the small win, a done plan step) fills with `CanvasText` and its tick takes the page's colour (`Canvas`), so ticked and unticked still differ. Checked by `tests/phase5/answers3.mjs` (chevrons and the close crosshair on the sprint page and the outside Health Check; the ticked parts in the design gallery). A switch and the eye's "shared" dot, which are two flat colours with no edge, get an edge and a knob in the system's colours there too (break-it tester, 12 Nov). Charts, bars and week squares are not yet drawn for that mode. Hover and focus edges on outlined parts use `--coral-text` or `--text-2`, never a colour fainter than the edge at rest.

9. **Spacing uses only the scale --space-1 to --space-7 (4px, 8px, 16px, 24px, 32px, 48px, 80px).** Tap targets are at least 44px (var(--tap-min)). No arbitrary spacing values.

10. **Motion uses only the --ease curve and the --dur-fast (150ms), --dur (250ms) and --dur-slow (400ms) timings.** Animate only transform, opacity, colour and size. No bounce, spin or fly-in effects. Everything switches off under prefers-reduced-motion. Planned movements include: drawer slide with scrim fade, crosshair rotation when a section opens, smooth expand of the Your Mission pill, sliding highlight in segmented controls and tab bar, switch knob glide, modal fade and rise, dropdown fade, button hover lift, cross-fade between dark and light, page fade between routes.

11. **Logo files in public/brand are used as supplied and never redrawn, recoloured, stretched or cropped.** The files are lockup-full_colour-on-dark.png (full logo with wordmark for desktop header), mark_colour-on-dark.png (emblem/mark for phone), and crosshair_white.png / crosshair_black.png (mask images for CSS masks).

    The logo mark always includes its crosshair. Light backgrounds: Emblem Logo POSITIVE (black crosshair, `public/brand/logomark-positive.png`). Dark backgrounds: Emblem Logo NEGATIVE (white crosshair, `public/brand/logomark-negative.png`).

17. **Every page sits inside .ui-root.** Never put global rules on html, body, #root or bare element selectors (input, button, textarea, select). (Three exceptions: the root text size in `src/index.css`, so text follows the device's text-size setting; the safe-area insets on #root in `src/pwa-standalone.css`, so nothing sits under the notch or the Dynamic Island: the sides on every touch screen, all four as a Home Screen app (the top bar adds the top inset itself in the browser); and the sticking table headings' rule on the page body, `body:has(.ds-ctable, .year-tbl, .plan-table) { overflow-x: clip; }` in `src/components/design/tables.css`, so a table's heading row can stick while the page scrolls (the coach portal's twin, `body:has(> #root .ui-root.cc-app)` in `src/pages/coach/coach.css`, is the same exception; tidy follow-up 3).) All design variables (--bg, --text, --coral, etc.) and component classes live only inside .ui-root and its data-theme variants. Everything a page opens, including pop-ups, panels, toasts and overlays, renders inside .ui-root too: if it attaches to document.body, give that element the .ui-root class so it sees the design variables.

18. **Every free-text box uses the shared steady text box (TextBox in src/components/design)**, with focus mode for long writing on phone. It grows to show all of its text with no hidden text, no scrolling inside the box and no max-height cap; the page scrolls instead, and the page never jumps while typing. AutoGrowTextarea is retired.

19. **Database timestamps are UTC with no time-zone ending on the live database.** Always read a saved created_at/updated_at (or similar) through parseDbTimestamp (src/lib/dateFormatter.js) before turning it into a Date, formatting it, sorting it or comparing it - never new Date(value) directly. Never use toISOString().split('T')[0] to get "today"'s date; use getLocalDateKey instead, which is built from the device's own getFullYear/getMonth/getDate. Times are shown on a 24 hour clock with no am or pm (07:53, 19:26) in the device's local time, always through formatClockTime or formatDayMonthTime in src/lib/dateFormatter.js - nothing formats a time on its own.

20. **Tables and grids must never squash.** Use fixed column widths, keep headings to one line, let cell text wrap (overflow-wrap: anywhere) and let rows grow to fit their content - never let a column crush its text or force a sideways scroll. Stack into cards under ~600px instead of shrinking a table.

21. **No swear words anywhere.** Not in product text, not in file names, not in code comments, not in variable or component names. If one is ever found, rename/reword it immediately.

22. **Mock-up files are the source of truth.** Before building or changing any page, open and read the actual mock-up file and spec in full. Never build from a summary or from memory. Copy markup, CSS values, wording and behaviour from the mock-up. If the built page differs, the mock-up wins. If something cannot match, stop and report it instead of simplifying. Every build summary must list the files read and any differences left.

23. **Wording and spelling: always write The Clarity Portal with a capital T on The, everywhere it is named.** Never "the Clarity Portal" or "a Clarity Portal account", even mid-sentence, in headings, buttons, legal text, emails, aria-labels, titles and toasts. Code identifiers, file names, CSS classes, URLs, environment variables and database values are not changed.

The eye is the only who-sees icon; nothing uses a lock for who sees something.

Its button gives the 44px tap area with padding; the icon never grows. When an "i" explains a sentence it sits at the end of that sentence, 6px after the last word (var(--info-gap)), lined up with the middle of the text. It opens its explanation and Escape closes it.

A stale tab's later save is still checked, so it is refused and shows the finished item. When the database doesn't answer (no answer, 502, 503, 504, 52x, PGRST000 to PGRST003, a statement timeout), the queue backs off (Tim, 5 Nov): about 2, 4, 8 seconds and on, up to about 5 minutes, with ±20% randomness; new saves wait in the queue meanwhile, a hidden tab never retries, and showing the tab or the browser's online event tries once at once (`backoffDelay` in syncQueue.js, `tests/performance/sync-backoff.test.mjs`). Being offline, or a failed sign-in refresh, never signs anyone out and never clears the device; only a deliberate Sign out does (`clearAppStorage`). **Sign out clears every saved copy of the person's on the device** (tidy follow-up 3), own questions, the day's plan order, peak moments and travel plans included (`PERSONAL_KINDS`), after sending across what still waits in them (`src/lib/signOutCopies.js`; "Some changes haven’t synced yet." when something can't go). A new storage key is either cleared there or listed with its reason in `tests/performance/sign-out-clears.test.mjs`, which reads every key in src. Errors never show raw technical text (`friendlyError`); a lasting problem adds "If it keeps happening, let your coach know." (or "use Something not working? in My Account." without a coach; `ErrorMessage keepsHappening`).

**Works on a flight (Phase 4 step 8).** A service worker keeps the app itself on the device, so the portal opens from the Home Screen with no signal. It is hand-written (`src/serviceWorker/worker.js`) and finished at build time by `vite.config.js` (`serviceWorker`), which puts the list of every file of the portal's build and of `public/` (fonts, brand images, icons) in front of it as `/sw.js`; registered only in production builds (`src/lib/serviceWorker.js`, from `startUpdateChecks`), never in development. Every portal page opened falls back to the saved `index.html`. It never keeps the Quick guide's videos and captions (left to the network: about 11 MB, and a browser asks for a video in pieces), nor database or sign-in answers (data offline comes from `pageCache` only), and it leaves the outside Health Check (`health-check.html`, `/health-check/*` and everything its page asks for), `/api/`, the design reference, the addresses a sign-in email link or Google comes back to (`/auth/google`, `/auth/confirmed`, `/auth/invite`, `/auth/reset-password`, and the older `/auth/callback`, `/auth/mission`, `/email-confirmation`, `/check-email-confirmation`), `/version.json` and `/sw.js` to the network. The Chinese font parts PDFs use are kept only once used. After sign-in and once per online visit, `src/lib/warmDevice.js` quietly reads the key pages' data with their own loaders into their device copies (Home's day, the plan, the sprint, Daily plan, Daily review, this week's debrief, My Plans, My Reviews; one at a time, nothing under 10 minutes old, never while saves wait). A page never opened, with no copy yet, says so with `LoadError` ("This page needs a connection the first time it opens on this device, so try again when you’re back online."). What truly needs a connection says so in one shape, `needsConnection` in `friendlyError.js` ("Signing in needs a connection, so try again when you’re back online."; pass the words as `friendlyError`'s third argument). The queue waits for a refreshed sign-in before sending and tries again on its own after a refused refresh (10, 20, 40, then every 60 seconds). A new version installs quietly and waits; it takes over on the next full open, or (`appUpdate.js`) when the page is hidden and nobody is typing, no dialog is open and nothing is being sent; drafts and waiting saves are on the device, so they survive it. Manifest `start_url` and `scope` stay "/". Check it with `tests/phase4/offline.mjs` (two production builds on the test project, served like Vercel by `tests/phase4/serve-build.mjs`).

**Text size follows the device** (Claude's call, 26 Oct): every text size is a multiple of `--u` (`calc(17 * var(--u))`), never a fixed px size, so text grows with the browser's text-size setting and, on iPhone and iPad, the Text Size setting (the one allowed global rule on html, in `src/index.css`), up to twice the standard size. Big display text (Nekst titles, big numbers, anything 20px and up) uses `--u-big` and grows by at most a quarter, so spaced capitals never split mid-word; the phone tab bar's labels stay 10px. Spacing, icons and tap areas stay fixed; layouts must wrap rather than break at the largest size (`tests/phase0e/app.mjs` checks every page at twice the standard size).

## Browser tests are silent

Every browser a test starts is muted (Tim, 16 Nov): `tests/mute-audio.mjs`, loaded by `tests/scores/register.mjs` (Chromium `--mute-audio`, Firefox volume 0, WebKit media held at volume 0), and by `test:panels` and `test:typing`. A browser started any other way passes `--mute-audio` itself (`scripts/make-quick-guide.mjs` does).

## Scores

**Every score is built from `docs/scoring-methods.md`, one function per section in `src/lib/scores/`, and every worked example is a test** (`npm run test:scores`). The evidence behind each claim is in `docs/claims-register.md`.

## Migrations and the rules test

- **Every migration from 025 on ends with the line that records itself**: `select public.record_migration(26, '026_what_it_does');`. `public.migration_log` lists what has run on each database; `supabase/MIGRATION_LOG.md` is the same list as Tim's checklist. Migrations are safe to run twice and are dry-run first with `bash scripts/dry-run-on-test.sh FILE.sql [TEST.sql]` (test project only, always rolled back).
- **`supabase/migrations/024_baseline_live_structure.sql` is live's structure**, and `supabase/024_baseline_test.sql` checks a database against it. A later migration that changes something in the baseline adds it to `later_version` (or `later_addition`) in that test.
- **`npm run test:rules`** checks every personal table's rules for each test account and for someone signed out (`tests/rules/`). Every new table, role and rule in Phase 1 is added to `tests/rules/config.mjs`, and the test must pass before Tim runs the migration.
