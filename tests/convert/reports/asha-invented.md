**Role mission:** Build a sales team that grows without me in every deal

**In short:** 3 Strategies; 3 habits, 1 to-do, 1 milestone, 1 objective measure, 1 kept as history. 1 to check.

## Strategy: Grow through referrals

- **Habit:** Referral conversations
  - Why: A number done again and again ("Referral conversations", per week), so it becomes a habit counted each week.
- **Objective measure:** Revenue from referrals
  - Why: A result to move ("Revenue from referrals", £), so it becomes the measure of an Outcome Objective, starting at 120000 with a target of 250000.
- **To-do:** Email three past clients
  - Why: A one-off action ("Email three past clients"), so it becomes a to-do in its Strategy.

## Strategy: Coach the team to close

- **Habit:** Weekly one-to-one with each rep
  - Why: Something done every week ("Weekly one-to-one with each rep"), so it becomes a habit: yes or no once a week.
- **Milestone (to check):** Hire a second account executive
  - Why: A one-off result with an end point ("Hire a second account executive"), so it is proposed as a milestone. If it is a step towards a bigger result, it may be a to-do instead.

## Strategy: Sustain my energy

- **Habit:** Training sessions
  - Why: A number done again and again ("Training sessions", sessions), so it becomes a habit counted each week.
- **History:** Book a holiday
  - Why: Already done ("Book a holiday"), so it stays in the old plan's history and isn't planned again.
