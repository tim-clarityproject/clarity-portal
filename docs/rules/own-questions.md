# Your own questions (mock-up "Your own questions", version 3; Phase 6a run 7, 22 Nov 2026)

The run's prompt wins over the mock-up, the design reference (design-reference-22nov.html) wins over the mock-up for how a part looks, and CLAUDE.md wins over both. Rules and words: `src/lib/ownQuestions/model.js` (`tests/performance/own-questions.test.mjs`); kept by `src/lib/ownQuestions/store.js` (device first), read back by `tidy.js`; screens in `src/components/ownQuestions` and `src/pages/coach/SuggestQuestionPanel.jsx`; the database is migration 055 (`supabase/055_own_questions_test.sql`).

1. **Up to 2 questions of a person's own in the Daily plan, the Daily review and the Weekly performance debrief, set only in My Account** (the section "Your own questions", after Settings, `id="own-questions"`). The mock-up's "Plan my day" and "Review my day" are the Daily plan and the Daily review (rule 26a). Each tool shows "1 of 2", its questions (the words, then "Written", "Yes or no" or "A rating, Never to Always" in grey; the whole row opens it), and the Add row "Add question" while there is room. A waiting suggestion takes the free place.
2. **Editing opens inside the row** (rule 29k), with the cursor at the end: "What’s your question?" (one line, the role's example inside), the tool's help line under it, "How will you answer it?" as the gliding switch (Written, Yes or no, A rating), and for a rating "Which words fit your question?" in the branded drop-down (a panel from the bottom on a phone) with the six five-word scales, How often first. It **saves by itself** a moment after typing pauses (a choice at once) with "Saved"; an empty new question is simply not kept. "Delete this question" deletes at once with "Deleted" and Undo (writing rule 14).
3. **120 characters at most** (the database too), with a count only in the last 20. Unseen characters are taken out (sign-in rule 38's way).
4. **The worry line** (writing rule 52): a Daily plan question with stress, worry, anxious, nervous or dread in any form changes the help line to "The Daily plan looks ahead, so questions about stress or worry work better in the Daily review." Advice only, the same quiet grey, never a block.
5. **In the tools they come last, just before the summary, one per screen**, with the small grey line "Your own question" above the words as the heading. Written has Next, which works with nothing typed (no Skip). Yes or no and A rating move straight on when tapped (rule 21), with "Skip this one" under the answers (a step's `skipLabel` in `GuidedTool`). The % bar counts them because they are steps; **someone with no own questions gets no steps and no summary section: the tools are exactly as before.** The questions are taken as the tool opens, so one deleted or added in Settings never moves the screens under someone answering.
6. **Summaries:** the day's answers at the end under the section "Your own questions", the question in grey and the answer under it, each under the words it was answered with; a skipped question doesn't appear. The weekly debrief shows its own questions' answers for the week, then the week's daily ones on its **working days only** (the sprint's M0.3 days): a rating as the answer given most ("“Often” on 3 of 5 days."), a yes or no as a count ("Yes on 3 of 5 days."), written answers as "You answered on 4 of 5 days." with "Read your answers" opening them in place (rule 29aq), each day's under its own words.
7. **Editing never rewrites past answers:** each answer keeps the words, kind and scale it was given under (`own_question_answers.wording`). A deleted question's past answers stay in their summaries, the day's and the week's. In the weekly debrief a yes or no or a rating edited mid-week gives one row for its earlier words and one for its new ones, so an answer is never counted under words it wasn't given to (written answers stay one row, each day under its own words in Read your answers).
8. **Scores:** a rating maps to 0, 25, 50, 75 and 100 (methods 1.5; stored by the database as `score`), but nothing reads it yet: no Performance score, streak or pattern uses own questions.
9. **Coach suggestions:** "Suggest a question" in the More fold on a client's page (only for a client with an account, not past), the side panel as the mock-up draws it: Which tool is it for?, What’s the question?, How will {first name} answer it?, "{first name} chooses whether to add it.", and "Suggest it to {first name}". It reaches the client's Settings as "Suggested by {coach's first name}" with Add it and Not now; with 2 already there Add it opens the two questions with Replace beside each. Not now tells the coach nothing: the coach never reads a suggestion back. A newer suggestion from the same coach for the same tool takes the waiting one's place. Home shows "See the question {coach} suggested" once (`HomeOwnSuggestion`, marked seen as it shows), opening My Account at it; never inside a tool. Names always come from the data. Suggesting needs 055 and a connection, and says so.
10. **Who sees:** a coach reads a client's own questions and answers only where the client already shares that tool: the weekly debrief, one week at a time by that week's switch. The Daily plan and review are never shared. Coach access needs the 6-digit code (054).
11. **Offline and before 055:** adding, editing, deleting and answering are kept on the device at once (`clarity-own-questions-local:<person>`, which Sign out never clears) and sent through the offline queue; before 055 they stay on the device with no error and move across once. Two tabs can't make a third question: the device refuses it, and the database refuses one from another device (that row then says it wasn't kept, with Delete this question).
