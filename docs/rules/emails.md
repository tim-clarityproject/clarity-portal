# Emails (Tim, 10 Nov; mock-up email-template.html, approved 14:26)

Moved from CLAUDE.md on 22 Nov 2026 (Phase 6a run 7, step 0), word for word. These rules apply exactly as they did there; CLAUDE.md points here.

1. **One shared email template for every email the portal sends** (`src/lib/email/template.js`). An email is described as a small document (`src/lib/email/emails.js`: its heading, lines, one button, any sign-in box, its footer) and the template turns that one description into both the HTML and the plain-text version, so the two never differ. No email builds its own HTML. What people type is always shown as text and kept to one line in a subject and in the plain text; every link must be an https address (or Supabase's and Kit's own placeholders in their saved templates, or `http://localhost` with its port on a developer's own machine), or the email is refused, not sent. Parts: the header, the Nekst button, the sign-in box, a quiet link, section labels, the quote and question, the "New" section and the footer. **Polish 1 (Stage C, 26 Nov):** the parts are the reference's: the one button is the black button (below), never the Nekst one.
2. **Always light** (many email apps force their own dark mode). The email's colours are the design reference's light values, written once in `EMAIL_COLOURS`. Built for Gmail, Outlook and Apple Mail: tables for layout, every style inline, no script, no web font relied on (Nekst for buttons and section labels and DM Sans for reading load where the app allows, with Helvetica and Arial behind every font).
3. **Portal emails** carry the bold crosshair (made from the one shared drawing by `scripts/make-email-images.mjs`, never redrawn) with "The Clarity Portal" beside it as text. **Emails from the business** (Leading Ideas) carry The Clarity Project's real horizontal logo in black (`public/brand/the-clarity-project-*.png`, the designer's files, never redrawn, recoloured or cropped). The image travels inside the email (an inline attachment, `api/_emailImages.js`), so it shows whichever deployment sent it.
4. **One coral button at most, with only a time beside it** ("~2 minutes"); on a phone it runs full width with the time under it. Leading Ideas has no button and nothing to answer; on the public list its one coral is the underline of "Create your free account" in the "New" section. **Polish 1 (Stage C, 26 Nov):** the one button is **the reference's main button, black with white words in DM Sans 16px at 500, 12px corners** (`template.js`; M28 v6 "email"), never coral; `tests/email/emails.mjs` checks it black with white words. The agreement email reads "Here’s the agreement for your six months of coaching, starting on Monday 4 January." after the note (`readyToSignEmail`'s `what` and `startsWords`, from `emailWords` in `questions.js`). The four Supabase templates were remade (`node scripts/make-email-templates.mjs`): Tim pastes them into Supabase again before go-live, or the sign-in emails keep the coral button. Coral stays only as Leading Ideas' "Create your free account" underline, for Tim to say.
5. **The footer** says why the person gets the email, then "**The Clarity Project LTD, founded by Dr. Tim Dutton**", whoever the coach is (`EMAIL_SIGN_OFF`). Leading Ideas reads "Leading Ideas by Dr. Tim Dutton, every Thursday." instead. **No postal address in any portal email, and never a home or family address anywhere.** The public Leading Ideas shows only the address saved in Kit (Kit's own `{{ address }}`), and nothing goes to the public list until a non-personal address is saved there.
6. **Health Check emails to the person show their email address and their access code in the sign-in box** (Tim, 10 Nov, 13:31): "Email my link", and the new code after Only you (045). **The answers email to the coach who made the link shows the person's email and never a code** (Health Check follow-up 1, 15 Nov), so nothing the coach is sent opens answers the guest later makes Only you. The owner's note about another coach's link never holds the answers, the link, the address or the code. Each email keeps its trigger, its link and who it goes to (`api/_routes/alignment-email.js`). **A Health Check email's link is built only from an allow-list of the portal's own addresses** (`api/_portalOrigin.js`; 11 Nov): the live site, this deployment's own preview addresses (Vercel's `VERCEL_BRANCH_URL` and `VERCEL_URL`, on a preview only) and localhost on a developer's machine. It is never built from the request's host: a forged host gets the live address (`tests/healthcheck/origin.test.mjs`). The email to the person never has "Talk it through with Tim" or any booking link (Tim, 10 Nov, 23:53). With no organisation name the subject is "A Health Check has finished", never one that starts with a space.
7. **Times in an email follow the time zone rules below**: in the reader's own zone with a city or country name ("10:00 am Chicago time", "15:00 UK time"), in the subject too; 12-hour for readers in the US and Canada; dates in words; both days when they differ. The day-before reminder's line "Travelling? Your calendar invite moves with you." shows only when the coach has ticked that person as a frequent traveller. **The 1-hour reminder gives its clock time with the place name** ("Your coaching session with Tim starts at 15:00 UK time"; draft-2-emails.md, approved by Tim on 23 Nov 2026, replaced the older rule that it gave none).
8. **Templates only, nothing sends them yet** (booking and Kit come in Phase 6a): the session reminders (the day before and 1 hour before) and Leading Ideas for clients and for the public list. "Choose which emails to get" needs its page first; until then the templates take the link as a setting and the emails that send today leave it out.
9. **The sign-in emails are sent by Supabase** from templates saved in Supabase, not in this code. `node scripts/make-email-templates.mjs` makes them (confirm, reset, change of email and invite, each with the link that opens in any browser: sign-in rule 42; and Kit's frame) from the shared template into `email-templates/`, for Tim to paste in; `--check` (a unit test) fails when they are out of date.
10. **The hidden email test page** is `/email-preview` (`src/pages/emailPreview`): every email with invented people, at 600px and 375px, with its plain text. Nothing links to it, and like the design gallery it exists only in local development and on previews (`src/main.preview.jsx`), never on the live site. Check every email with `node --import ./tests/scores/register.mjs tests/email/emails.mjs [folder for screenshots]`; add a new email to `src/pages/emailPreview/examples.js` so both pick it up.
