# Team performance check and the Visual tools' shared parts (mock-up M10, version 5.6; Tim, 13 Nov)

Moved from CLAUDE.md on 22 Nov 2026 (Phase 6a run 7, step 0), word for word. These rules apply exactly as they did there; CLAUDE.md points here.

M10 wins for these. Checked against M10 itself, part by part to half a pixel and word for word, by `tests/phase8/compare-m10.mjs` (Desktop 1440 and Phone 390, M10's own phone frame, dark and light; each meant difference is listed there with why), with the flows and the UI tester's measurements in `tests/phase8/team-check.mjs` and the rules in `tests/performance/team-check.test.mjs`.

1. **The Team performance check is one guided tool at `/team-performance-check`**, on the Interpersonal hub under "Your team" (`src/pages/teamCheck`, rules in `src/lib/teamCheck/model.js`): Who reports to you (at least one person), then three one-tap questions for each person (trust, alignment, autonomy; "Person 1 of 5" above each, the name in curly quotes), then **Your team**. It is `GuidedTool` with `calm`; the % bar counts questions (`percent`), and the results have no bar (`noProgress`). **Polish 1 (Stage B, 26 Nov):** it runs in GuidedTool's focus mode (CLAUDE.md rule 29o's note); Finish later sits in the foot after Next, where Skip this step would, not at the bottom bar's left; "Your team" and the summary headings are 20px DM Sans questions, no longer Nekst titles. Coral is for mistakes only now (CLAUDE.md Polish 1), so rule 6's coral Alignment bar is drawn in the text colour; `tests/phase8/team-check.mjs` checks that.
2. **Trust is Tim's five levels, lowest first:** Silent resistance, Open resistance, Uncommitted, Token cheerleader, Committed, each with its line of meaning (`TRUST`; an answer's `hint`). **Alignment and autonomy are five-word scales** (Not well at all to Extremely well; None at all to A great deal), said back as sentences ("Priya understands what you’re trying to achieve very well."). Never a number, never "freedom".
3. **Your team opens with one sentence, never a big figure** ("1 of your 5 people is in the high-performance area right now."), then the chart, "Show as a table", Trust, and **Questions to start with: last** (rule 29ar; its colon is Tim's). The parts are shared: `ResultsHeadline`, `ResultsSection`, `StartQuestions`.
4. **The chart** (`TeamChart`): alignment up, autonomy across; three areas named in place (High-performance in mint, Permission seekers and Chaotic in grey; the fourth has no name); one small dot with the first name for each person; a dashed ring where they were at the last check. **Chart dots are never coral.** **No name comes within 4px of an area's name** (Phase 8a follow-up, 17 Nov): a name that would goes to the other side of its dot, or just above it, kept inside the drawing (`chartDots`' `nameUp`, `chartFrame`'s `labels`; every spot checked in `tests/performance/team-check.test.mjs`, and on screen at 400 and 360 by `tests/phase8/team-check.mjs`). No key: every dot has the shared pop-up (`ChartTips`) and a 44px tap area at any width.
5. **"now" and "At your last check, in October"** (the real month of the last finished check, with its year when that isn't this year; `lastWords`), never "this month" or "last month". A new check starts from the last finished one's people with nothing answered, and keeps that check's answers inside itself (`last`), so an old check never changes when another is deleted.
6. **The colour rules live in one function** (`barColours`): coral marks the Alignment bar of anyone in Chaotic, or, with no one there, the lowest Alignment, ties included (never at 4 or 5). Autonomy is never coral, and a Chaotic person's Autonomy bar is grey. Trust: Committed is mint and the single lowest is coral (the first on the list when two share it). Coral is never on anything that can be tapped.
7. **The five fine steps are one shared part** (`StepBars`, `StepBarRows`; M7b's project list will use it): the answer is always there in words too. "Show as a table" under a chart can be a quiet link (`ChartTable` with `link`, and `rowHead` for a person on each row), "Hide the table" while open; it keeps your place (rule 29aq).
8. **Questions to start with:** at most three, Chaotic first, then trust (the biggest fall since the last check), then autonomy (a Permission seeker), each naming its person in 15 words or fewer (a very long name is said as its first word; `startQuestions`). A first check has no trust question: nothing has fallen yet. **With nobody in Chaotic and no trust question, every Permission seeker is asked about, up to three; with none, the section is hidden** (Phase 8a follow-up, 17 Nov; 5e rule 6).
9. **"Add a to-do" is the results' one coral button** and opens the shared add panel (`ToolTodoPanel`, rule 31: as M9 draws it, the Strategy in the branded drop-down and "How much" unchosen until chosen, "Choose Low, Medium or High to carry on."; the to-do's source is `other_tool`, its `source_id` the check). **"Download my PDF" is a text link** beside it (`PdfDownload` with `variant="text"`, in the step's `footAside`). **"Finish later"** is the quiet link at the left of a typing step (`skipLabel` with `skipMoves` false): it saves and closes.
10. **The eye shows on the first step and on Your team only:** Only you, or You and the coach, with "The people you rate never see it." (`WhoSees` with `plain` and `line`). The choice is kept with the check (`shared_with_coach`); no coach reads it yet.
11. **Saved checks are kept in `tool_answers`** (migration 043; `src/lib/toolAnswers.js`), device first: until Tim has run 043 they stay on the device with no error, and move across once. A check is a draft until every question is answered, and is saved as finished when the results show. In My Plans it reads "November team check", tool "Team performance check", under Interpersonal; a draft carries on from the list and from Home.
12. **Lists people build are rows of names** (`NameRows`): the crosshair removes a row with "Deleted" and Undo. The add panel never greys its button: "Type their name to carry on." Editing who's on the list from the results asks about anyone new before the results show again (a step's `onward`).
13. **Its PDF** (`src/lib/pdf/docs/teamCheck.js`) follows the same order: the headline in At a glance, the chart with its three areas named, the same answers as a table in words, trust (with "At your last check, in October" when there was one), and the questions last.
14. **A tool that is saved for good and leaves keeps no draft on the device** (14 Nov; Get unstuck, the Team performance check and the Emotional wash). Its page clears the draft with the draft hook's own `clear()` (`useDraft` in `src/lib/drafts.js`), never `clearDraft` alone: the page writes its draft once more as it leaves, so a finished item came back as one to "Carry on where you left off", and the next visit opened it again instead of a new one. Nothing may change what is on screen between that clear and leaving (a save on the way out passes `stays: false`). Each flow test checks no draft is left.
15. **Stage D (Draft 11, Tim's answers of 27 Nov):** "What's working well with [name] right now?" follows each person's three ratings and can be skipped; the autonomy question asks "Which decisions can [name] own, and at which colour?"; one commit question comes last ("What will you do first with [name], and when?", naming the person the questions to start with name first) and can't be skipped. **Decide who decides** (Green "They decide, act and report back.", Amber "They decide, and I confirm before they act.", Red "I decide.", word for word, `DECIDE_COLOURS`) shows after the questions on the results and in the PDF (an exception to rule 29ar's order, for Tim to confirm), drawn as three stacked lights: mint filled (Green), a mint ring (Amber), grey filled (Red), never coral.
