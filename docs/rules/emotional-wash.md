# Emotional wash (mock-up M10, version 5.6; Tim, 13 Nov)

Moved from CLAUDE.md on 22 Nov 2026 (Phase 6a run 7, step 0), word for word. These rules apply exactly as they did there; CLAUDE.md points here.

M10 wins. Checked against M10 itself by `tests/phase8/compare-m10.mjs` (every wash screen, Desktop 1440 and Phone 390, dark and light; each meant difference listed there with why), with the flows and the UI tester's measurements in `tests/phase8/emotional-wash.mjs`, the rules in `tests/performance/emotional-wash.test.mjs`, and both Visual tools at twice the text size in `tests/phase8/text-size.mjs`.

1. **The Emotional wash is one guided tool at `/emotional-wash`**, on the Reset hub after the Pressure plan, where M3 has it (`src/pages/emotionalWash`, rules in `src/lib/emotionalWash/model.js`): What happened, the kind of feeling (one tap), its words, what it could be related to (optional), the story, then four screens for each thought, then **Your wash**. Its tile's line and time are M3's ("Work through a strong emotion so it doesn’t run you.", "~10 minutes").
2. **The words are the ten difficult feelings of the Geneva Emotion Wheel, exactly as M10 has them** (`WORDS`): Anger, Contempt, Disappointment, Disgust, Fear, Guilt, Hate, Regret, Sadness, Shame. As many as fit, side by side (`MultiChoice` with `columns`), said back in the order chosen ("Disappointment, anger and contempt."). No finer words until one has a research source.
3. **No strength rating anywhere**, before or after (Tim, 2 Nov), so nothing can suggest the tool didn't work. Checked in the model's test, the flow and the PDF pack.
4. **One thought per box** ("Type one thought in each box.", `RowList`), with "See an example", which opens in place (rule 29aq). A box taken out says "Deleted" with Undo.
5. **True, logical and helpful are three screens for each thought**, each one tap (Yes, Not sure, No) with one line saying what it means, under "Thought 1 of 3" with the thought in curly quotes without its last full stop (`quote`). They repeat for every typed thought, then **How will you wash it?**
6. **The follow-up opens inside the chosen card** (5e 29k; the shared `FollowChoice` and `FollowQuestion`). **Park and reflect** asks "What’s the question that will be helpful to reflect on?" with three starters (`STARTERS`, the shared chips) that fill the box when tapped. **Accept and act** asks "What’s one small step you’ll take?" with "Where should it go?" in the step (`TodoWhere`, rule 31). **Trash** lifts the thought away letter by letter (the shared `Dissolve`: the tokens' timings only; with reduced motion the words simply fade), then "That thought has gone.", "It won’t appear in your summary." and "Deleted" with Undo ("The thought is back"). A finished wash saves no trashed thought.
7. **A step's to-do is made when Next is pressed on its screen** (a step's `onNext`; `useToolTodo`, source `other_tool`, `source_id` the wash), so the summary only says "It’s in this week’s to-dos." or "It’s in your backlog." when it is there (`whereLine`). An edit puts a new to-do in the old one's place; a to-do whose thought no longer wants one is taken away (`strayTodos`). Its toast ("Added to your backlog" with Undo) is never replaced by the edit's own "Saved", which lands after it now that 043 saves to the database (`toast.show('Saved', { keepUndo: true })`; Health Check follow-up 1, 17 Nov). **A to-do can't be saved without its Strategy**, so with more than one Strategy in the plan the card also asks "Which Strategy does it serve?" (M10 doesn't draw it; with one Strategy it isn't asked). Impact is Medium.
8. **Finish later is at the left of every typing step** (What happened, the words, related to, the story, each washing screen); one-tap screens and the summary have none. A button is never greyed out: each screen's `check` says what it needs ("Type what happened to carry on.", "Choose one to carry on."). **Polish 1 (Stage B, 26 Nov):** in GuidedTool's focus mode Finish later sits in the foot after Next, where Skip this step would; Your wash's headings are 20px DM Sans questions, no longer Nekst titles.
9. **The eye has no choice** (`WhoSees` with `note`, on the first step and Your wash only): "Only you can see this.", then "Your Emotional wash can’t be shared, like your Journal." Nothing of a wash is ever shared with a coach: it is saved with `shared` false, and migration 043 refuses anything else. In My Plans it reads "Your wash" (never what was typed), tool "Emotional wash", under Personal, and it never shows in a hub's recent list (`private` in `savedItems.js`). Home's "Carry on where you left off" shows the tool's name only.
10. **Your wash** is a mirror of what was named and chosen (`summaryRows`, `GuidedSummary` with `stacked`): What happened, The feeling (the kind, its words under it), Related to, To act on, To reflect on, each with Edit; several steps or questions get a row each. Edit beside The feeling opens the kind, then its words (a step's `thenEdit`). "Finish my wash" is the one coral button ("Saved"), with "Download my PDF" as the text link beside it.
11. **Its PDF** (`src/lib/pdf/docs/emotionalWash.js`) mirrors Your wash in the same order, with blanks and trashed thoughts left out and each step saying where it is.
12. **The % bar** reads M10's 0, 9, 18, 27 and 36 on the first five screens; the thoughts' screens share the rest evenly (`percentAt`), so it only ever moves forward.
