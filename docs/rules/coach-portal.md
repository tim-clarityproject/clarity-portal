1. **The coach area is `/coach`, open only to coach accounts** (`CoachApp.jsx`, `useCoachRole`: an active row in `public.coaches`; role `owner` is Tim, `coach` an associate). Coach sign in lands there, and Sign out goes back to `/coach/sign-in`. **What a page shows is never the protection: the database's rules are** (rule 9).
2. **Its own Home Screen app**: `public/coach-app/` (manifest, `favicon.svg`, `favicon.ico`, `apple-touch-icon.png`, `icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png`, `icon-maskable-512.png`; made by `scripts/make-coach-icons.mjs` from the one shared bold crosshair, black on a white tile at 69% of the square, 52% on the maskable one). `index.html` swaps the manifest, icons and name for any `/coach` address before anything is read. Never a folder called `public/coach`: that address is the portal itself.
3. **The frame** (M11): the header is the bold crosshair menu button, one search box and the bell with its small count; the slim next-call bar's slot under it on every page (from 10 minutes before a call until 10 minutes in, coral from 5 minutes before, Join on Zoom a quiet link: `nextCallBar`; it fills in run 3, and until then takes test calls on builds that aren't live, `src/lib/coach/calls.js`); the menu above everything; on a phone no header menu button, and the phone bar is Today, the menu and Calendar. **`__CLARITY_LIVE__`** (vite.config.js) is true only in the live site's build: pages of later runs (`later` in `src/lib/coach/navigation.js`) show in the menu and open the calm "Coming in this build" page everywhere else, never on the live site.
4. **No keyboard shortcuts are shown anywhere** (Tim, 18 Nov): no key hints in search, menus or pop-ups (M11 draws some). Escape, Enter and the arrow keys work, and are never listed.
5. **Every action has Undo in its message for 10 seconds**: `toast.saved({ message, onUndo, long: true })` (`TOAST_LONG_UNDO_MS`). Deleting a client's data is the one thing with no Undo: the name is typed first.
6. **Search** (`Search` in `CoachApp.jsx`, `searchResults`): one box finds people, pages and actions as it is typed, with recent items before anything is typed; each coach searches only their own clients, and the owner finds an associate's client by name only ("Maya’s client", which never opens). Each opening starts empty.
7. **Settings is two panes** (`Settings.jsx`, `SettingRow`): the sections on the left and the chosen one on the right; on a phone the list, and each section full screen with Back. Each setting is one calm row: its label, its value and Edit; a box shows only while editing, with Save and Cancel; switches for on and off. Associates never see Email, Note layouts, Coaches, the Activity log or cover. Built fully: Your details, Away and cover, Notifications, Coaches, Look, Tags and fields, the Activity log. Rows and storage only, wired in later runs: Booking, Working hours, Time zones, My calendars, Email, Note layouts.
8. **Clients** (`Clients.jsx`): list, board and table behind the gliding view switch; **the table only at 1100px and wider** (rule 20: a tablet and a phone offer the list and the board); every card on the board is one height (`useEvenCards`); column menus sort, show only and hide, and float above the table (the shared `Popover`, position fixed: `PopMenu`); custom fields are columns edited in place (`EditCell`: the grey edge, the cursor at the end, Enter saves, Escape puts it back); saved views, up to 5 in the menu; choosing several is a round tick on hover (inside the row on a tablet, press and hold on a phone), then Tag, Move (the owner) or Merge, each with Undo. Groups and workshops sit beside people as tabs; Past clients behind a quiet link.
9. **Who sees what is the database's** (049): an associate reads only their own clients; the owner reads only admin facts of an associate's clients (`coach_admin_clients()`: name, coach, organisation, status, the engagement and its sessions), never their notes, About them, files, tags or fields; a cover coach reads the client's name and what the client shares (`may_see`), only between the cover dates and after the client agreed; About them and private notes are the coach's alone; tags, custom fields and saved views belong to the coach who made them. A page only ever shows what its coach's own reads returned.
10. **Device first until Tim runs 049** (`src/lib/coach/store.js`, as peak moments and tool answers): every change is kept on the device at once (`clarity-coach-local:<account>`, which a Sign out never clears while anything on it hasn't reached the database); once the tables are there it is sent through the offline queue, what waited is moved across once (parents before their parts), and the device copy becomes only the last data shown (`clarity-cache:<account>:coach`). Tables a coach may add to but not change are sent as "add unless there" (`ADD_ONLY`); a notification is only ever marked read (`MARK_ONLY`). What needs two coaches or the database's own check (Move to a coach, approving a profile, adding a client for another coach) waits for 049 and says so (`NEEDS_049`).
11. **Nothing in the coach portal sends by itself.** Email opens the coach's own email app (`mailto:`); WhatsApp and iMessage show only with a mobile number, iMessage only for +1 (`quickContacts`). Invite to the portal only records that it was pressed. A name in a quiet line opens a drafted note (`NameNote`) with Email; **Portal message isn't offered: the client portal has no messages page to show one in.** Add coach asks `api/coach-invite.js`, which checks the caller is the owner and sends only from the live site.
12. **A link inside a sentence** (a person's name: `InlineLink`, `.cc-inline-link`): the line's own colour, a quiet grey underline 4px below; the words turn coral on hover and keyboard focus while the underline stays grey; a 44px tap area.
