# Sign-in pages (mock-up M26, version 4; Tim, 11 Nov)

Moved from CLAUDE.md on 22 Nov 2026 (Phase 6a run 7, step 0), word for word. These rules apply exactly as they did there; CLAUDE.md points here.

M26 wins over everything else for these pages. They are built only from `src/components/signin` (`SignInParts.jsx`, `signin.css`: M26's own values, each part from the design reference) with their rules in `src/lib/signIn`, and checked against M26 itself, part by part to half a pixel, by `tests/signin/compare.mjs` (all its screens, dark and light, 1280 and 390 by 844).

1. **The bold crosshair and THE CLARITY PORTAL sit at the top of every sign-in page, in the same place on every page** (`SignInPage`). The name is in Nekst capitals, nudged down 0.1em, with its middle on the crosshair's middle (within 0.5px). New brand work uses the bold crosshair: never a Made Studios lockup, never a new logo. **Polish 1 (25 Nov 2026 design reference and example pages; replaces the above where they differ, the rest stays):** the crosshair alone at the top (40px, `--si-mark`), `max(80px, 11vh)` from the top (`--si-top`); THE CLARITY PORTAL (Nekst 13px, 0.2em, grey) at the foot, 40px from the bottom (`--si-foot-pad`).
2. **Each page other than Sign in has one short title in capitals, as its h1**, word for word from M26 (Create your account, Check your email, Email confirmed, Reset your password, New password, Link not working). On Sign in the name is the h1, and "Clarity drives performance." shows there only. **Polish 1 (25 Nov 2026 design reference and example pages; replaces the above where they differ, the rest stays):** Sign in has the title "Sign in" as its h1, like every other page; there is no core line ("Clarity drives performance." is gone from it).
3. **One spacing rhythm:** 24px under the name or core line; 12px between boxes; 18px above the button; the first text link 18px under the button. No footer on these pages. **The spacing rule (Tim, 19 Nov, 21:25; M26 seq 7; Phase 6a run 5) wins where they differ, on every sign-in and account page:** title to its first line 12px, line to line 12px, text to a box, list or button 24px, box to button 18px, button to link 18px, paragraphs at 1.55 line height (`signin.css`; measured from the page's own boxes by `tests/signin/spacing.mjs` in `measure.mjs` and `coach-auth.mjs`; My Account's pages moved to it from M26's 22px and 17px, listed in `measure.mjs`'s `MEANT` and `compare.mjs`'s `SPACING_RULE`). `measure.mjs` and `compare.mjs` read M26 seq 7. **Polish 1 (25 Nov 2026 design reference and example pages; replaces the above where they differ, the rest stays):** the reference's rhythm: 12px between every part of the form (`--si-gap`), the title's own 20px under it (32px in all), 24px from the code squares to the link (the empty message line between); no footer rule (the name sits at the foot). The values live in `src/styles/tokens.css`.
4. **Text links stacked one above another never share a tap area:** each keeps its own 44px, so their lines are at least 28px apart. **No two tap areas overlap anywhere in the portal:** calm-rules fails any that do, on every page (`tests/phase5/taps.mjs`: a control inside another, a control drawn inside the box it belongs to such as "Show", parts on another layer and parts scrolled out of sight are not overlaps).
5. **One tick box:** "I’m 18 or over and I agree to the terms", where "terms" opens the one terms panel with all three legal documents (`TermsRow`). Its row lines up with the boxes' left edge. **Every tick box in the portal is the shared 18px one** (a 1.5px edge, 5px corners, a 12px tick, a 44px tap area: `.ds-check-box`); no page draws its own. **Polish 1 (25 Nov 2026 design reference and example pages; replaces the above where they differ, the rest stays):** the shared tick box is 24px with a 1px edge and 6px corners (A1's `--checkbox-box`), ticked filled in the text colour.
6. **Buttons are never greyed out.** Pressing Create my account or Continue with Google without the tick shows "Tick the box to carry on." beside the box. A message sits where M26 draws it: under the box it is about.
7. **Create my account asks for First name, above Email** (kept in the account's `first_name` and `profiles.first_name`, where the portal already reads it). An account set up from a coach's invite has no First name box.
8. **Passwords are at least 8 characters and never a known leaked one**, checked in the page as well as by Supabase (`src/lib/signIn/password.js`: only the first 5 characters of the password's fingerprint leave the device). "Please use at least 8 characters." shows only after a password that's too short is tried.
9. **Email sign-ups confirm their email before they get in.** Email confirmed ends with "Open my portal". **Reset links work for 1 hour**, and the page and the email both say so. A new password signs people straight in to Home, with "Saved".
10. **People always stay signed in on their own devices:** no "Remember me". No "email me a sign-in link". **Sign out ends only this device's sign-in** (Phase 6a run 6: `signOut({ scope: 'local' })` in `src/lib/supabase.js`, for Sign out and Delete my account); other devices stay signed in. Only the owner's Reset authenticator ends a coach's sign-ins everywhere.
11. **After signing in, people land where their link pointed, otherwise Home** (`safeNext`, `takeNext` in `src/lib/signIn/addresses.js`): only ever a place inside the portal.
12. **A return address is worked out from the page's own origin, never written out** (`returnAddress`). Supabase only returns to addresses on its allowed list; a missing one is added there by Tim, never worked around.
13. **Words, exactly as M26 has them** (`src/lib/signIn/words.js`): errors say what happened, then what to do, on two lines ("That email or password doesn’t match." then "Try again, or reset your password."); "Forgotten your password?", "Email my reset link", "Didn’t get it? Send it again", "Save my new password", "This link has run out or has already been used.", "Send a new link", "Set my password". Sign-in boxes stay labelled "Email" and "Password" (the agreed exception). Password boxes say "Show", never an eye. **Polish 1 (25 Nov 2026 design reference and example pages; replaces the above where they differ, the rest stays):** errors that take two lines stay in the boxed message; on Enter your code a wrong code is one small centred line under the squares, their edges in the mistake colour (`--error`). The words are unchanged.
14. **Every box has the right autocomplete** (username for Email, current-password, new-password, given-name for First name, one-time-code for the code) **and every label is tied to its box.** Hover and keyboard focus show on the Google button, the tick box, Show and every text link. **Polish 1 (25 Nov 2026 design reference and example pages; replaces the above where they differ, the rest stays):** the labels stay tied to their boxes but are hidden; the names show inside the boxes.
15. **The agreement to the terms is stored the same way however the account is made** (`src/lib/signIn/terms.js`): on the account itself (`terms_accepted`, `terms_accepted_at`, `terms_documents`, which migration 021 reads) and on `profiles.terms_accepted`, which `AuthContext` fills in from the account's own record on the next sign-in.
16. **Tests never send a real email and never open Google's own page:** `tests/signin/shared.mjs` answers every call that would (sign-up, resend, reset, a change of email).
17. **Continue with Google sits above email** on Sign in, Create my account, the coach's invite and Coach sign in. It is Google's own button (`GoogleButton`): their four-colour G, "Continue with Google", their light version in light and their dark version in dark, never recoloured. It comes back to `/auth/google` on the page's own origin ("Signing you in", `GoogleReturn`). **Polish 1 (25 Nov 2026 design reference and example pages; replaces the above where they differ, the rest stays):** Google sits under Sign in and "or" as a quiet outlined button (the buttons' hairline), DM Sans 16px, the boxes' height (56px, 52px on a phone), Google's own four-colour G at 18px, 12px before the words; the same order on Create my account, the invite and Coach sign in (the tick box comes before Google there). Google's own light and dark buttons and Roboto are no longer used.
18. **A Google account the portal has never seen agrees before it gets in.** From Create my account with the box ticked, the tick is carried through Google's round trip (this tab only, 15 minutes) and saved on the way back. From Sign in there is no tick, so it gets **Before you start** ("You’re signing in as …", the tick box, Create my account, "Use a different Google account"), then Home. Google sign-ups skip First name, because Google gives the name. The portal stays shut until the box is ticked: `ProtectedLayout` asks `signInStop` (`src/lib/signIn/flow.js`) and sends them back, whichever page they open.
19. **Same email, Google or password: one account, never two.** Supabase joins a Google sign-in to the account that already has that confirmed email; the portal never asks for a second one. An account that began with a password and adds Google later is not asked to agree again (`needsBeforeYouStart`).
20. **Google cancelled by the person: no message, just back to the page they pressed it on. Google not working:** back there with "That didn’t work." then "Try again, or use your email and password." **No connection:** one calm line, "Signing in needs a connection, so try again when you’re back online."
21. **A deep link survives Google's round trip** (kept for the tab while Google is open). The page asked for is read from the router in `ProtectedLayout`, never from the address bar, which has already moved on when a redirect runs twice.
22. **Google's return is tested without Google:** `tests/signin/google.mjs` answers the hand-over to Google as its return, for a new account, a Google account the portal knows and an email account with the same email. Never drive Google's real page in a test, and never type a real Google password.
23. **Coaches have their own sign-in page at its own address, `/coach/sign-in`** (`SignIn` with `coach`): the title "Coach sign in", Continue with Google above email, and no Create my account link. After the password or Google it asks for the 6-digit code from their authenticator app (`/coach/sign-in/code`, "Enter your code": Supabase's own TOTP sign-in factor). **Google never skips the code**, and neither does the clients' page: `signInStop` holds any account with a verified authenticator at the code page until it is given, and `ProtectedLayout` keeps the portal shut meanwhile. The code box has no example code in it, and "6-digit" never splits across lines (`.si-nowrap`). **Polish 1 (25 Nov 2026 design reference and example pages; replaces the above where they differ, the rest stays):** Enter your code reads "From your authenticator app. It goes in by itself." (`src/content/pageLines/signin.js`).
24. **The coach authenticator (Phase 6a run 5; M26 seq 7; migration 053): a coach with no authenticator is taken to set it up after the password and can't reach the coach portal until it's done** (this replaced "let in after the password"). `signInStop(supabase, user, { coach })` asks `coachAuthStop` (`src/lib/signIn/coachAuth.js`, every rule and word) on the way into the coach portal (its address, or Coach sign in's own landing), and `ProtectedLayout` holds every `/coach` address, so typing the address never skips a step: Set up your authenticator (`/coach/sign-in/set-up`: the three apps each in its own 44px row; the QR drawn here from Supabase's own enrolment address, dark dots on its own square of the light page colour (`--si-qr-paper`) in both modes, the 4-module quiet zone included and no card or edge round it, so it reads without inverting (the prompt's light on black was judged a real risk for Google and Microsoft Authenticator, so the code alone got the page-coloured square, as decided: the UI tester, 21 Nov); the key in fours with a small copy icon and "Copied." in the shared toast; Code and Turn it on), then Save your recovery codes (`/coach/sign-in/recovery-codes`: 10 codes two to a row, Download as a text file named in words, the copy icon, "I’ve saved them somewhere safe.", and "Open the coach portal", which stays unavailable until ticked: the prompt's one exception to "never greyed out"; Back returns to set-up). The set-up key and the codes live only in the page's memory: never logged, kept or sent anywhere but Supabase. Enter your code says "You’re only asked on a new phone or computer."; a wrong code "That code didn’t work. Check the time on your phone and try again."; 5 in a row wait 30 seconds, said calmly (the 6-digit count kept on the device, so a reload or a new tab doesn't skip it; the recovery codes' count in the database, 053). The tick names its set (053's `coach_recovery_codes_saved(set)`): a set another tab replaced is never ticked as saved ("These codes stopped working…"), and once a set is saved, Back or the codes address typed never makes a new one. A sign-in from before the owner's reset changes nothing in 053 (`signed_out`), and the coach portal asks again what a coach owes as they move between its pages and come back to the tab, at most every 10 seconds (`ProtectedLayout`'s `RECHECK_MS`), so a tab left open is let go too (the break-it tester, 21 Nov: `tests/signin/coach-auth-breakit.mjs`, `supabase/053_coach_authenticator_breakit_test.sql`). "Lost your authenticator app?" (`/coach/sign-in/help`) takes a recovery code and names the owner from the data (never email); You’re in (`/coach/sign-in/in`) gives the count and Set it up, and the 2-or-fewer line shows once after a code sign-in. My Account's Sign-in security (`/my-account/sign-in-security`, coaches only) needs a current code before Set up on a new phone or Make new codes. The owner's Reset authenticator is in each associate's More menu on Settings, Coaches (a side panel, through `api/_routes/coach-auth-reset.js`); a sign-in from before a reset is signed out on Coach sign in with the reason. Clients never see any of this. Recovery is never email alone (Tim, 10 Nov, 23:24). **Using a recovery code ends the coach's other sign-ins and keeps the one that used it** (run 7; the lost phone may be in someone else's hands): the database refuses any sign-in begun before the code other than that one (055's marker, `others_ended_at` and `kept_session`, asked by `_coach_auth_session_ok` and `coach_code_ok()`), removes their sessions where it can, and the page asks Supabase to sign out the others (scope others). You're in says "Your other sign-ins have ended."; an ended sign-in is told on Coach sign in that a recovery code was used elsewhere (`coach_auth_state`'s `ended_by`). **Polish 1 (25 Nov 2026 design reference and example pages; replaces the above where they differ, the rest stays):** the code is six squares (48 by 56px, 44 by 52px on a phone, 24px digits) over one hidden input with `autocomplete="one-time-code"`; Enter your code has no Sign in button, the sixth digit sends it (Enter still sends fewer, which says what's missing). Set up and Sign-in security keep their buttons and also send on the sixth digit. Any refusal empties the squares.
25. **Inside My Account** (`src/pages/signin/AccountPages.jsx`, in the portal's own frame): **Password** (`/my-account/password`: Current password, New password, "Save my new password", then "Saved" and the boxes clear) and **Email** (`/my-account/email`: "Save my new email", then "Check your email" with two links to open: rule 43). A new email only takes over once both links are opened. The current password is checked without touching the device's own session (`checkCurrentPassword`), so a coach keeps the code they gave. **Someone who signs in with Google has no Password or Email page:** My Account says "You sign in with Google." instead. The links read "Edit my email" and "Edit my password", never "Change".
26. **No test enrols a real authenticator on any test account** (Phase 6a run 5). `tests/signin/coach.mjs` signs the test coach in for real and stands Supabase's MFA in; `tests/signin/coach-auth.mjs` walks every authenticator screen and flow stood in whole (`coachWorld.mjs`: nothing reaches the test project), reads the QR back with jsQR (pinned, dev only), and the owner's reset as the real test coach with 049 to 053 and the server stood in. The coach portal's own suites show the test coach as one with an authenticator who has given its code (`tests/signin/aal2.mjs`, used by `tests/coach/shared.mjs`'s `coachContext` and `standIn`: a stand-in factor, the page's copy of the token at aal2, and the real token on every request to the test project). `tests/signin/flows.mjs` covers signing in, creating an account, passwords and My Account's pages.
27. **Measured on every sign-in page** by `tests/signin/measure.mjs` (all 21 screens, dark and light, 1280 and 390 by 844): the name's middle on the crosshair's middle and both in the same place on every page; Back's chevron with its visible edge on the content's left edge, 10px before its label (`--back-gap`, Tim, 18 Nov, follow-up 1), centred on its words and clear of a phone's notch; the spacing rhythm read the same way on the page and on M26 itself; links 28px apart with no overlapping tap areas; the tick box; "6-digit" on one line; every outlined edge at 3 to 1. A heading on these pages sets `word-spacing: normal`, because an older rule (`src/index.css`) widens the gaps between every heading's words and M26 doesn't. A test browser rounds a 1.5px edge to whole pixels when asked what it drew, so the tick box's edge is checked as written.

28. **A page that sets a password without asking for the old one opens only for the account a link has really just brought** (the functionality tester, 13 Nov). New password needs a reset link opened on this visit (`useLinkSession`'s `fromLink` and `kind`: Supabase's own "reset link opened" event, and the link's code taken out of the address), or one opened in this tab in the last hour by the same account. The invite's page needs an invite link, or an account a coach set up that hasn't agreed yet (`invitedNotSetUp`); anyone else signed in is passed on in. A session already on the device, or an address made to look like a link (`?code=`, `?token_hash=`, `#access_token=`), opens neither. Never decide this from `status === 'ready'` alone.
29. **Email links can carry their own token** (`?token_hash=…&type=recovery|invite|signup|email_change`): the landing page finishes it itself, once, and tidies the address. This is the only form an invite can take (nobody's browser asked for it), and the form every saved template uses (rule 42): a link in Supabase's standard form (`{{ .ConfirmationURL }}`) only finishes in the browser that asked for it; anywhere else a reset link shows Link not working and a confirmation link shows Email confirmed, then Sign in.
30. **An account with an authenticator app gives its code before a new password is saved** (Supabase refuses it otherwise): New password shows Enter your code first, on the reset link's own address.
31. **A carried tick agrees only for the sign-in it was ticked for** (`tickAgreesFor`): from Create my account or the invite, for an account that hasn't agreed. Continue with Google from any other page clears an older tick (`continueWithGoogle`'s `ticked`).
32. **Sign out leaves nothing of the sign-in pages in the tab** (`clarity-sign-in-*`, cleared by `clearAppStorage`), and the page it was pressed on is not kept as the place to land (`noteSignedOut`, `justSignedOut`): the next person to sign in starts at Home.
33. **Someone only half in who opens Sign in or Create my account starts again** (`useAlreadyIn`): held at Before you start or at Enter your code when the page opened, that half sign-in is let go on this device, so they can use another account. Someone fully in is passed on as before.
34. **One request per press:** Sign in, Create my account, each Continue with Google, and every save or send on these pages ignore a second press while the first is on its way. `tests/signin/links.mjs` checks rules 28 to 32 in the browser.
35. **A page left open never saves for whoever signed in since** (the break-it tester, 13 Nov). New password and the invite's page ask, just before they save, whether the device's sign-in is still the account their link brought (`stillSignedInAs` in `linkRules.js`): the sign-in can change under an open page (another tab), and a save goes to whoever is signed in now. If it has changed, New password shows Link not working and the invite's page passes on. A page the browser brings back whole (Back, after another site) once the person has signed out, or someone else has signed in, starts again (`AuthContext`'s `pageshow`).
36. **A place to land is checked as it comes out, not only as it goes in** (`safeNext`): "/.//other.site" reads as "//other.site" once its dots are taken out, so anything that ends up starting with two slashes is refused.
37. **The code box reads a code however it arrives** (`digitsOf` in `CoachCode.jsx`): "123 456" and "123-456" pasted or put in at once, and full-width digits, all read as the six digits. The box still holds 6 at most.
38. **Passwords are 72 characters at most** (Supabase keeps no more): "Please use 72 characters or fewer." (`tooLong`, counted in bytes, so an emoji counts for more than one). Passwords are never trimmed; a first name of only spaces or of characters nobody can see is no name (`cleanName`), and an email with an unseen character in it is refused in the page.
39. **"Doesn’t match" only when Supabase says so** (`invalid_credentials`): any other refusal on Sign in gets "That didn’t sign you in." Every request on these pages gives up after 15 seconds with a calm line, the check of the current password included (`checkCurrentPassword`).
40. **Sign out counts for the person's other tabs too:** for half a minute none of them keeps its page as the place to land (`leftJustNow`; only the time is kept on the device, as `clarity-left-at`).
41. **What only the page enforces, and what Supabase does** (stated plainly, 13 Nov). Supabase enforces: who can read and write which rows, the password rules, one account per confirmed email, that an account with an authenticator gives its code before a password change, and every link's one use. The page alone enforces: Before you start (the tick before the portal opens), and the authenticator code before the portal's pages open. Someone who edits their own browser's storage can open the portal's pages without them, and then sees only what their own sign-in may already read. **The database asks for the coach's code (migration 054, run 6; Claude's call, 21 Nov):** every coach table and coach function answers a coach only once that sign-in has given its 6-digit code (`aal2`, `coach_code_ok()`), so a stolen password plus direct calls to the database gets nothing of the coach portal; the coach server functions ask the same (`api/_coachCode.js`). Before the code a coach still reads their own coaches row and what the sign-in screens need (053's functions, their own authenticator rows); a recovery code only lets them set up a new authenticator, which is what raises the sign-in to `aal2`. Clients are unchanged.
42. **Email links open in any browser (Tim, 13 Nov, 14:05).** The four templates saved in Supabase (confirm, reset, invite, change of email; `email-templates/`, made by `scripts/make-email-templates.mjs`) carry the link's own token: the page it lands on (`{{ .RedirectTo }}`; the site's own `/auth/invite` for an invite, which no page of the person's asked for), then `?token_hash={{ .TokenHash }}&type=…` (`tokenLink` in `src/lib/email/template.js`; only those exact shapes pass `safeHref`). Never `{{ .ConfirmationURL }}`, which only finishes in the browser that asked. The landing page hands the token in itself, once (rule 29). A link that lands anywhere but the page that finishes its kind is put on that page before the app reads the address (`linkPage` in `linkRules.js`, `toItsOwnPage` in `linkSession.js`; the functionality tester, 13 Nov): the site's root, where Supabase sends a link when the page that asked isn't on its allowed list, or an address with a doubled slash, from a Site URL saved with a slash on its end. A return address never carries a query of its own (the link adds `?token_hash=`). A link handed in while the database gives no answer (no connection, 502 to 504, 52x) is handed in once more before anything is said: that is not a link that has run out (the break-it tester, 13 Nov). Because the link now carries the whole token, **Supabase's list of allowed return addresses is what keeps a reset link from being sent to someone else's page**: it must hold only the portal's own addresses, never a pattern that another site could match (Tim's to check in Supabase; nothing in the code can).
43. **A change of email has two links** (Supabase's "Secure email change" is on: Tim, 13 Nov). After Save my new email the page's title is "Check your email" and it reads "We’ve sent a link to your current email and to {new email}. Open both to finish."; nothing else on it changes. The one email goes to both addresses, so its words fit either reader. The link lands on `/my-account/email`, which is inside the portal, so its token is handed in as the app loads, signed in or not (`emailChangeLink` in `linkSession.js`): the second link opened shows "Saved" there; the first shows "That’s one link opened. Open the one sent to your other email to finish." (on Sign in when that browser isn't signed in); a link that has run out says so and what to do. The first link opened where someone else is signed in says so on their Email page too, and signs nobody in or out. Once the email has changed, My Account shows the new one straight away: the app's copy of the account (`AuthContext`) keeps its place for the same account except when its email is newer, and the Email page reads the account only after the link is finished. How the link went is said once (`emailChangeToSay`), "Saved" only to the account whose email changed and never beside something typed since. My Account's Email and Password pages start again when the device's sign-in changes under them (each is keyed by the account), so nothing typed, sent or waiting for one account is ever shown to, or sent for, another (the break-it tester, 13 Nov).
44. **An invite never makes a second account.** On the invite's page Continue with Google is joined to the invited account (`continueWithGoogle`'s `joinTo`: Supabase's `linkIdentity`, which needs "Allow manual linking" switched on in the project), never signed in on its own, and Google opens on the invite's own address. If the Google account that comes back has another email it is taken off again (`googleMatches`, `unlinkIdentity`) and the page stays, with "This invite is for {email}. Continue with that Google account, or set a password." under the Google button. Every Google account with another email is taken off, one left by an earlier try included, and the right one counts beside it (`googleStrangers`). With manual linking off, Supabase refuses before Google opens and the page says "That didn’t work." by the tick box; setting a password still works. The tick box comes first, exactly as on Create my account ("Tick the box to carry on."). The emails must be the same letter for letter once trimmed and A to Z lower-cased (no dots dropped, no "+tag", no look-alike letters), and a Google email marked unconfirmed never matches. A wrong Google account can still be left joined when the tab is closed on the way back or taking it off misses, so it is taken off again whenever the account is finished (the invite's Set my password, Before you start: `takeOffStrangers`), and a Google sign-in from any other page that lands in an unfinished invited account with no Google account of its own email is taken off, signed out and sent back with "That didn’t work." (`cameInByStranger`). Continue with Google on the invite's page, and Create my account on Before you start, first ask that the device is still signed in as the account the page names (`stillSignedInAs`), as a password save does (the break-it tester, 13 Nov).
45. **An invited account that hasn't agreed never skips the tick box, whichever way it comes in** (`needsBeforeYouStart` covers it as well as a new Google account). A reset link shows Before you start first, on the link's own address, then New password; signing in any other way is held at Before you start by `ProtectedLayout`. "Use a different Google account" shows only for a Google account.
46. **A device clock that's wrong is said calmly on Sign in** (and Coach sign in), above Continue with Google: "Your device’s clock looks wrong, which can sign you out. Set it to update automatically, then sign in again." It shows when the device's clock is more than 5 minutes from the server's, read from the Date of a normal request for the portal's own version file (`src/lib/signIn/clock.js`). No coral, no icon: the page's grey words (`Note`, `.si-note`), a status, not an alert. When the server's time can't be read, nothing is said. A copy served from the edge carries an Age, and Vercel writes the time now as its Date all the same, so the server's time is taken to lie anywhere from Date to Date plus Age and only a clock outside that by 5 minutes is told (`serverTimeFrom`); never add the Age to the Date. The check runs again when they come back to the page, so the line goes once the clock is right. A test never sets the page's clock ahead of the real one while signed in: Supabase then signs the page out after about 30 refreshes. The device's time is taken as the whole span from sending the request to its answer, so a slow answer never looks like a wrong clock; only a Date written as servers write it counts; an answer that is refused, passed on to another address, or 15 seconds late says nothing; and one question is asked at a time (the break-it tester, 13 Nov).
