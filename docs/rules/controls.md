# Controls: one family (Tim, 22 Nov 2026; design reference 22 Nov; Phase 6a run 7, step 2)

Read with CLAUDE.md rule 4a, which still holds every button, switch and field size.

1. **The gliding switch is the one toggle.** Every choice between views or answers is the soft grey track (`--seg-track`) with 8px corners and the chosen option a raised tile with 6px corners (`InlineChoice`, `SegmentedControl`, `PillChoice`; the `.ds-seg` classes). The round pill toggle (`.seg`) is retired everywhere, M1's round No/Yes and rating presses (`PressRow`) and M11's pill search box included. Tapping the chosen option where it could be cleared still clears it.
2. **Underlined tabs stay for moving between parts of a page** (the sign-up terms panel's three documents), never drawn as pills.
3. **On/off settings use the one quiet precision switch**, 40 by 24, mint when on (`.ds-switch`, `SwitchRow`).
4. **Every Add that starts a new item is the Add row** (`AddButton`, rule 29).
5. **The hidden measuring copy of a switch is taken out of the flow** (`.ds-seg.ds-seg-measure:not(#_) { position: absolute }` in `buttons.css`); a gliding switch sits on its box's left edge within 1px unless the layout centres it.
6. **The lasting check** is `controlsFamily({ phone, scale })` in `tests/buttons/buttons-check.mjs`, run on every client page by `tests/phase5/calm-rules.mjs`, on every coach screen by `tests/coach/ui.mjs`, and on the pages those two don't open by `tests/buttons/controls-sweep.mjs` (read-only; every write answered locally). Its own tests: `tests/buttons/controls-family.test.mjs`. It fails a round pill toggle, tabs drawn as pills, a track whose corners aren't 8px or whose tile isn't 6px, a track that isn't 40px high (44px on a phone; taller only when larger words wrap or to match a box beside it), a switch that isn't 40 by 24 and mint when on, a pill with words inside a control, anything drawn as a button that isn't 40/44px high with 8px corners, Nekst inside any control, and more than one coral button on a screen's top layer.
7. **Left to their own rules** (each with a comment in the check): underlined tabs, menu and drop-down items, quiet text links that switch one thing by their words, answer rows, chips (6px), form boxes, tick boxes, week day boxes, chart dots, the Breathe orb pill, the My Mission pill, Continue with Google (sign-in rule 17), and Merge into one's comparison cells.
