# Get unstuck, the shared timer and guest pages (mock-up M9, version 12; Tim, 13 Nov)

Moved from CLAUDE.md on 22 Nov 2026 (Phase 6a run 7, step 0), word for word. These rules apply exactly as they did there; CLAUDE.md points here.

M9 wins for these. Checked against M9 itself, part by part to half a pixel and word for word, by `tests/phase8/compare-m9.mjs` (Desktop 1440 and Phone 390, dark and light; each meant difference is listed there with why), with the flows in `tests/phase8/get-unstuck.mjs` and the rules in `tests/performance/get-unstuck.test.mjs`.

1. **Get unstuck is one guided tool at `/get-unstuck`** (`src/pages/getUnstuck`, rules in `src/lib/getUnstuck/model.js`): Goal (needed), What’s in the way, What’s already working, Options with the timer, Which options will you act on (at least one), What exactly will you do, then Your plan. The old step addresses redirect to it with their query, hash and state (`GET_UNSTUCK_STEPS`). It saves in `decisions` as `grow`, in the old pages' shape plus the lists and next steps (`savedForm`, `planFrom`), so old saved plans open on the new screens; a saved plan opens on Your plan (`?id=` or the page's state). Closing part-way saves a draft and keeps the place on the device.
2. **The calm stepped frame is `GuidedTool` with `calm`** (M9, M10, M7b): the bars line up with the page's column, Back shows on the first step too (it leaves the tool), no % figure, the step's `label` is the stage name, Skip is a quiet link at the left. **A button is never greyed out:** a step's `check` returns the calm line ("Type your goal to carry on."). Your plan is `GuidedSummary` with `stacked`; rows are `RowList` with `removeAlways` and `undo` ("Deleted" with Undo); next steps are `NextSteps` and `NextStepRow`. "Save my edit" goes straight back to the summary and says "Saved" (`onEdited`). A tool's PDF is "Download my PDF", the quiet link at the left of its summary's bottom bar (`footAside`; M10), in all three tools. A panel's one button sits at the right on a laptop and runs full width on a phone (`.ds-panel-go`).
3. **On a stepped tool the eye shows on the first step and on the summary only** (Tim, 13 Nov, 09:32), never on every step. Its label ends "Edit who can see this." For one saved item it is `WhoSees` with `plain` (Only you, or You and the coach; kept in the item as `shared_with_coach`); a guest's is `WhoSees` with `note` ("Your answers go to Tim, who sent you the link.").
4. **Each next step says where it is:** "It’s in this week’s sprint." (mint), "It’s in your backlog." or "It isn’t in this week’s sprint yet.", with "Add to this week" (or "Edit") opening the one add panel (`ToolTodoPanel`, rule 31: This week, Backlog, Don’t add it). Finishing lands on This week with the counted message ("1 next step is in this week"; "Saved" when none is).
5. **One timer, the same everywhere (`Timer`, Tim, 13 Nov, 09:27):** a 64px ring with the time, one outlined button (Start the timer, Pause, Carry on, Start again) and Reset once started. Tapping the ring before it starts or while paused opens "How long?" (4, 6, 8 or 10 minutes, or Custom: a Minutes box, 1 to 60, "Set the timer", "Type a number from 1 to 60."); one tap sets it, closes the panel and says "The timer is set to 8 minutes." The ring can't be tapped while running. The length is remembered per person (`person_settings.page_preferences.timer_minutes`, the device copy first; on the device only for a guest). It counts to an end time, so a hidden tab never puts it wrong. No tool builds its own.
6. **Guest pages** (`/guest/<token>`, `src/pages/guest`, `GuestFrame`; migration 035's functions through `src/lib/guest`): the bold crosshair alone in the bar, its drawing on the column's left edge like everything under it, and centred only on a short message page (one left edge, sign-in rule 49; Tim, 23 Nov 2026, 17:40); they follow the device, light or dark (`boot.js`), and the service worker leaves them to the network. The start shows the email already filled in when the link carries it (`?email=`) and asks only for the 6-character code in one plain box. The tool is the same one, with no sprint lines ("start soon"). The end offers "Plan this into your week with The Clarity Portal, your personal performance tool.", "It’s free, and this plan moves into it.", "Create my free account" (never a booking link), the PDF and Delete my answers ("Deleted" with Undo, which saves them again). The visit key stays in the tab (sessionStorage), never in the address. Until 035 has run every call says one calm line. `guest_claim` is asked once after each sign-in (`src/lib/guest/claim.js`), so a guest's answers move into the account with the same confirmed email. **The guest chooses who sees their answers** (Health Check rule 12; Tim, 14 Nov, 09:52): the eye on the first step, on Your plan and on the end page offers Only you or You and the coach, You and the coach by default, saved on the link (044) and in the saved item (`shared_with_coach`). This replaces the 27 Oct rule that a guest's answers always go to the coach.
7. **"A copy is on its way to {email}." shows only once the server has sent it** (`api/guest-copy.js`, migration 042): the browser sends only its visit key, the address and the answers come from the database, and **nothing is sent from a preview or a developer's machine** (`maySend`: production only). **The copy goes only to the address the link was made for** (046, `guest_link_create(tool, email)`): never an address typed by whoever opens the link; a link made without one sends no copy.
8. **Tests answer the guest functions with stand-ins shaped as the SQL** (`tests/phase8/shared.mjs`), never a real link, and block every `/api/` and email call.
9. **A saved plan, check or wash is deleted from inside it** (the functionality tester, 14 Nov): opened from My Plans, Get unstuck, the Team performance check and the Emotional wash show the quiet "Delete" (`SavedItemActions`) under the summary, and under the first question of a draft; **it deletes straight away, with no "Delete this?"** (writing rule 14; Phase 8a follow-up, 17 Nov), then back to My Plans with "Deleted" and Undo, which puts the same row back with its own id (`deleteSavedItemWithUndo` in `savedItemDelete.js`; My Plans and My Reviews read their lists again, `useSavedItemsChanged`). The same for every item opened from My Plans or My Reviews, the stop-doing audit's own page and the inside Health Check's versions and teams. Nothing of it stays on the device. Opened any other way there is no Delete.
10. **"Save my edit" saves there and then** (Get unstuck, a guest's page, the Emotional wash): the item is written as it stands (a finished one stays finished, one still being made is a draft), so "Saved" is true on every device. A plan saved by the old pages keeps the name its owner gave it.
11. **A toast never sits on a guided tool's button:** where the bottom bar's button runs under the middle of the screen (a phone), toasts rise to 16px above the bar, as M9 draws them (`GuidedTool`); dialogs do the same for their footers.
12. **Before migration 043 has run, a check or a wash lives only on the device.** A deliberate Sign out sets those aside under `clarity-tool-answers-local:<person>` (never cleared, like the other answers that wait for their migration) and the person gets them back on signing in again on that device (`clearAppStorage.js`, `toolAnswers.js`); everything the account already holds is cleared as before. Deleting the account clears them too. **Once 043 is there (since 16 Nov on the test project), what waits on the device moves across once, and Sign out sends what still waits first (`sendToolAnswersBeforeSignOut`; anything that can't go counts as a waiting save, so Sign out asks before leaving it), then clears everything of these tools from the device: nothing is set aside any more** (`clearAppStorage`'s `toolAnswersThere`; Phase 8a follow-up, 17 Nov; checked by signing out at the end of `tests/phase8/team-check.mjs`).
13. **035's messages are shown with the portal's own apostrophe**, and a closed link says "This link has closed. Ask Tim to send a new one.", with the coach's first name when the link has one, otherwise "your coach" (`closedLine`; Phase 8a follow-up, 17 Nov) (`openProblem`, `guestWords`): coming back with an email has no page yet. **A guest who opens their link again (a new tab, another device, the next day) goes back to their answers** (migration 046; Phase 8a follow-up, 17 Nov): while they share with the coach, the link, their email and the code bring back that link's earlier answers; once they have chosen Only you, the coach knows the code, so the page asks for a 6-digit code emailed to the guest's own address first ("You chose Only you, so your answers show once your email is confirmed.", "Show my answers", "Didn’t get it? Send it again"; 035's `guest_email_confirm`, sent by `api/guest-copy.js` kind `code`). Never an empty tool. Until 046 has run, a reopened visit starts a new plan, as before.
14. **People who share a spot on the Team performance check's chart stay in their own area:** side by side while they fit on their side of the dividing line, otherwise in rows one above another, never off the drawing (`chartDots`).
15. **Whatever a device kept, the screens get something they can draw** (the break-it tester, 14 Nov). A draft, a guest's tab copy or the device's copy of saved checks and washes that was damaged, or kept by another version, opens as far as it can be read and never stops the page: `tidyPlan` (Get unstuck), `tidyCheck`, `tidyWash`, `tidyRecords` (`toolAnswers.js`) and `readVisit` (a guest's tab). A new tool that keeps a draft reads it back through a function like these, never by spreading what was kept over an empty one.
16. **A next step's to-do follows its words until it is ticked, and goes with its option** (Phase 8a follow-up, 17 Nov): editing a next step in Get unstuck changes its to-do's words too (`useToolTodo`'s `follow`, which reads the to-do first and leaves a ticked or deleted one alone), and un-choosing an option deletes its next step's to-do with "Deleted" and Undo, which brings both back in their place (`remove`). **A tool says where its to-do is now, not where it once put it.** A next step's line ("It’s in this week’s sprint.", "It’s in your backlog.") is checked against the sprint page's own rows (`todoPlaceNow` in `sprintPage.js`; `useToolTodo`'s `placeOf`; `ToolTodoPanel`'s `todoIds` and `onPlaces`): deleted, done in an earlier week or in a sprint that has ended, it reads "It isn’t in this week’s sprint yet." and the old to-do is forgotten, so adding it again never touches it; moved to the Backlog or back, it says so. Nothing is second-guessed while a save still waits to be sent, or for a to-do made since the tool opened.
17. **Two tabs never undo a finished item.** Once a plan, check or wash has been finished in one tab, another tab still showing it unfinished saves nothing when nothing was changed there, and keeps what was changed as an item of its own (a new id), never over the finished one (`GetUnstuck.jsx`'s `mine`, `finishedInAnotherTab` in `toolAnswers.js`). A change the page makes itself (rule 16) is passed as `{ quiet: true }` and doesn't count as the person's.
18. **Nothing empty is finished:** Your plan asks for the goal ("Type your goal to carry on.") and the wash's summary opens the first screen still to answer, whatever place the device kept.
19. **The timer keeps the length it started with** until it is reset, whatever is chosen in another tab or arrives from the account meanwhile; a second tap within 400ms is ignored (it started and paused in the same moment); and a device clock put back while it runs never starts it over. A clock put forward ends it early, as a laptop waking does: it counts to an end time.
20. **A guest's answers survive their visit ending.** When a visit ends while the tab still holds the answers (12 hours) and the same email opens the link again, the answers stay on the screen and are saved as a new row (035 lets a new visit neither see nor change the earlier one's). A save that didn't go is sent again when the connection is back. Delete my answers asks once per press.
21. **What a guest can save is limited in the database** (035, 14 Nov): a row is at most 200,000 bytes, a guest holds at most 200 rows in a table ("That’s too long to save. Shorten it and try again."; "That’s as many as can be saved with this email. Delete one you don’t need and try again."), and a guest's email is one address only: one @, a dot after it, none of `, ; < > ( ) [ ] \ " :` (`_guest_email`; `oneAddress` in `api/_guestEmails.js` checks it again before sending). **The copy email never carries more than the page could have written:** 300 characters for the goal and for each next step, 50 next steps (`copyContent`).
