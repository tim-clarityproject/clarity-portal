# Travel plan (mock-up M21, version 7.2; Phase 8b part B, 18 Nov)

Moved from CLAUDE.md on 22 Nov 2026 (Phase 6a run 7, step 0), word for word. These rules apply exactly as they did there; CLAUDE.md points here.

The Phase 8b brief wins over M21, and the rules in this file win over both. The engine is `src/lib/travel` (`plan.js`, `docs/travel-plan-method.md`, `tests/performance/travel.test.mjs`); the screens' rules are `src/lib/travel/model.js` (`tests/performance/travel-screens.test.mjs`), kept by `src/lib/travel/store.js`; the flows are `tests/phase8/travel-plan.mjs` (1440, 768 and 400, dark and light; `SIZES`, `MODES`), and the pages are in calm-rules (`travel-*`).

1. **One tool at `/travel-plan`** (`src/pages/travelPlan`), on the Plan hub under Personal ("~3 minutes"): Start ("Where are you flying next, Sam?", Plan my trip, the peak moments in another time zone, Saved plans), set-up at `/travel-plan/new` (`?moment=` from a peak moment, `?again=` Use again, `?edit=` Add or remove a key moment), Your plan at `/travel-plan/:id`, a day at `/travel-plan/:id/day/:date`. Plan, day and short pages are the calm guided frame with one step each (`GuidedTool`; a step's `foot` is its own bottom bar, `null` for none).
2. **Set-up asks only** the flight out, the flight home (filled in as the way back), the key moments, then the check with usual sleep. From and To search airports by code, name, city or country, then the time library's cities (`airports.js`, `PlaceField`); Leaves and Lands are the device's own date and time picker. **Key moments are peak moments in the trip's time zone**, already chosen (never one kept at home); "Add a key moment" makes a new peak moment there (work, Perform at my best). A key moment added here whose day the flights later leave behind is asked again ("Choose a day in your trip for “Keynote” to carry on."), never saved outside the trip. Usual sleep is the person's own (`page_preferences.travel_sleep`), else 23:00 to 07:00: no check-in keeps a bedtime yet, so the panel never says it came from one. "Make my plan" is the one coral button; "Making your plan", then "Your plan is saved." (it saves as it's made). Buttons are never greyed out ("Choose when it leaves to carry on.").
3. **Your plan**: "Here’s your plan for New York, Sam." with the eye, the dates, "Do the steps that fit your days. Each one helps.", then Your body clock (`BodyClockChart`), Day by day, Your trip (Edit on each flight and key moment, each in the side panel), "Add or remove a key moment", "Use again" once the trip is over, and "Delete this plan" ("Deleted" with Undo). **The read-out** works by hover, touch scrubbing (`touch-action: pan-y`) and the arrow keys; it leads with the day's key moments, then "Body clock ~80% adjusted to New York time" or "~60% back on London time"; no date (the axis label is lit). Once under way, Day by day starts with today and earlier days fold under "Show earlier days".
4. **A day**: each row is the time and the step, key moments in time order; only a day across two time zones labels every time with its zone (a key moment with no time reads "All day", with no zone), a day away says "Times are New York time.", a day at home says nothing. "Why these steps help" opens the side panel (Light, Caffeine, Sleep, a key moment; then the medicines line) with **"I don’t have caffeine"** at its end: every caffeine step goes everywhere (days, Home, the Daily plan, the panel), with Undo; "Show caffeine steps again" brings them back. **A short trip** (2 nights or fewer, or 2 hours or less) is one page on home time; its panel opens with "Staying on London time". **Going west, sleep moves later just enough that getting up is never before 05:00 and lights out never after 01:00 local time** (Tim, 18 Nov, 09:00; `sleepMove`, `RULES.shortWakeFrom`, `shortBedBy`), the steps' other rules kept, with one line under the headline giving the real time it would have been: "Your sleep moves a little so you’re not up at 02:00." Going east nothing moves (London to Dubai keeps its 02:00 lights out). **Caffeine in the air is allowed** (Tim, 18 Nov): a last caffeine during a flight reads "Have your last caffeine by 14:00, on the plane." (`caffeineWords`, the step's `onPlane`).
5. **Editing a flight or key moment reworks the plan from today** from where the body clock is; the days before stay exactly as they were (`past`, kept on the device). A key moment's new day moves its peak moment ("Saved. Your plan and your peak moment are updated."; "peak moment" in lower case, as everywhere in the portal), and a peak moment moved anywhere else moves the plan by itself. **A repeating peak moment's day is never moved from the plan** (moving it would move every time it comes round): its panel keeps the day ("It’s one of your peak moments and it repeats, so its day stays Tue 10 Nov.") and offers its start time only. The chart's day labels never touch: the lit day's neighbours step back (`chartDayLabels`).
6. **Where it shows.** Home's offer row, once, 1 to 7 days before a peak moment in another time zone with no plan ("Plan my New York trip", the solid grey plane, `OfferRow icon="plane"`; Not now: "We won’t ask again for this trip."; one trip at a time). During the trip, Home's "Today’s travel plan" card (`TravelTodayCard`): the day's three steps in time order with private tick boxes, never what wasn't ticked. The Daily plan's "What will you do today?" shows the same steps above the list without tick boxes, with "Open today’s travel plan" (`TravelDayPanel`). My Plans lists each plan ("New York trip", Travel plan, Personal).
7. **Who can see this**: "Only you can see your plan.", then "[Coach] sees that you’re away in New York, and your key moments." The plan, its steps and its ticks are never shared; only the away flag (`travel_away`: city, zone, dates, hours apart) is written for the coach portal (Phase 6a builds the coach's view).
8. **Kept device first until Tim runs 048** (`clarity-travel-plans-local:<person>`, never cleared by Sign out), then sent through the offline queue and moved across once: `travel_plans`, `travel_flights`, `travel_plan_moments` (with the key moment's `starts_at`), `travel_ticks`, `travel_away`. As with peak moments, only a save the database has answered is marked sent (a queued one once a load finds the tables holding exactly it, `planKey`), and a refused plan stays on the device. 048 takes every flight the app takes (up to 30 hours: landing from 2 days before to 3 days after the day it leaves). The steps are never stored: the engine works them out each time.
