The Phase 8b brief wins over M18 where they differ, and the rules in this file win over M18. Rules in `src/lib/peakMoments/model.js` (`tests/performance/peak-moments.test.mjs`), kept by `src/lib/peakMoments/store.js`, flows in `tests/phase8/peak-moments.mjs`, and the pages are in calm-rules (`peak-*`).

1. **A peak moment is "a moment that needs more of your energy, attention and focus, because of its cognitive, physical or social demands."** Its page is `/peak-moments` (`src/pages/peakMoments`): the title with the eye and **no ⓘ**, the quiet link "What’s a peak moment?" opening the shared side panel (`ExplainLink`), Coming up then Past (a past one can't be tapped and shows its lesson; a repeat that has stopped shows its last time there, and one kept with no day it comes round on at all can be opened, to put it right or delete it), one `AddButton`; with none, the calm card "When do you next need to be at your best?" with "Add my first peak moment" and "~1 minute" beside it. No tile (M3 has none): it sits under the sprint's routes and is reached from the sprint page, Start a sprint, View your year and Home. **No limit on how many, anywhere.**
2. **Add a peak moment is the calm guided tool, one question a screen** (M18 draws one side sheet; the brief wins): What is it called?; Is it for work or your personal life? ("It’s for work", "It’s for my personal life"); What do you need from it? ("I need to perform at my best", "I want to be fully present", "I want to arrive at a break rested"; short names Perform at my best, Be fully present, Taper before a break); When is it? (one day, or first and last day); **Where is it?** ("It’s at home" already chosen, and a city search giving the city and its IANA zone: `src/lib/peakMoments/places.js`, on the one time library; a device's older zone names read by their current ones, Kolkata, Kyiv, Ho Chi Minh City, `currentZone`, and zones are compared that way everywhere, `sameZone`; Delhi and Mumbai found too; the time's name under a city only where it adds something, "India time" under Kolkata, never "New York time" under New York); Does it repeat? (It doesn’t repeat, Every week, Every month on this date, A set day each month, Every year, with an optional "When does it stop?"); then **Your peak moment**, one row per answer, the whole row opening its question, "Save my edit" going back (and saving a saved one there and then: "Saved"). Names are kept as sign-in rule 38's `cleanName` keeps them (unseen characters out, so a name of only those is no name; `cleanMomentName`). A stop day before the start is never kept on any path: moving "When is it?" past it lets it go, and Finish and "Save my edit" both refuse one (`isComplete`). Toasts: "It’s in your peak moments"; "Deleted" with Undo. A repeat is a rule (`repeat_rule`), never a list of dates; each time it comes round (an occurrence, `id:YYYY-MM-DD`) has its own lead-up and review; a month without the day is skipped, never rolled on (29 Feb is found however far ahead, past 2100). A review's address is only a day the moment comes round on (`comesRoundOn`); any other shows "This peak moment isn’t here any more."
3. **Private by default.** The page's eye shares work moments with the coach (`shareWork`, each work row's `shared`); a personal one is never shared ("Personal peak moments are only ever yours."). 048's rules let a coach read only shared work moments, and refuse a personal one marked shared (`supabase/048_peak_moments_and_travel_test.sql`).
4. **Where they show.** Start a sprint: a skippable step "When do you need to be at your best?" before Ready (its Add comes back to the step; the earlier steps keep their % figures). The sprint page: a section titled "Peak moments" (on the sprint page "in this sprint" is understood; a title is at most three words), only when the sprint holds one, with `PeakWeekBars` (M18's 8 week bars; **one ring per week, two or more as one larger ring with the number**; tapping fills it and lists the week's moments under the chart, with no pop-up and no focus box; keyboard focus is the coral ring; each ring's tap area is 44px or more and **the nearer ring wins**, `nearestRing`, where M18 let the later one win). Sprint details lists them. View your year: `PeakYearList`, a plain list by month with the sprint that holds most of it; a moment beyond the running sprint says Waiting.
5. **The lead-up (the 3 days before) and the review after.** Home: **in the 3 days before, the countdown wins the one card, except on the weekly debrief's own day (the last day of the week it is for), when the debrief keeps its turn** (Tim, 18 Nov, 09:00; `debriefsOwnDay` in `home.js`); on the day itself the card takes its turn (once the day is planned, in place of "Ready to log today?"): "Cup final, 3 days to go", one button **Plan for it** (the Pressure plan, still the older page), and Not now ("We’ll ask again tomorrow."). **On the day it reads "Cup final is today". HOOK: once the Pressure plan is rebuilt, a moment's own Pressure plan takes this card's place on the day** (`homeCard`'s `onTheDay`). Daily plan: one question straight after Sleep, in the kind's own words, with full-sentence chips; the answer becomes one of today's to-dos (`todos.source` 'peak_moment' once 048 is there, 'other_tool' before, `source_id` the moment); at most one a day (the nearer moment; two on one day: perform first, then by name); never in Recovery or on a break. Daily review asks "Did you do it?" in one tap, which ticks that same to-do. The review after is offered on Home from the day after (after a break, the day after it *starts*) for 3 days: "How did “Cup final” go?", "Were you as present as you wanted to be?" or "Did you arrive rested?" (one tap), What helped your energy and focus most?, What would you keep or change for next time?, then Your review with **what was done in the lead-up at the end only**; "Save my review" says "Saved for your next peak moment" and keeps the lesson on the moment. **On a break (from its second day) Home shows no work prompts.** Countdowns read "3 days to go", never "left". Phone notifications don't exist yet, so none are sent.
