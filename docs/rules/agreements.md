# Agreements, quotes and invoicing (Phase 6b run 3, 25 Nov 2026; mock-up M28 version 4; migration 065)

The run's brief (`phase-6b-run-3.md`, 46 items) wins over M28 where they differ, and these rules win over both. Read with coach-portal.md (rules 5, 9, 11, 22, 49, 50), pdf.md, emails.md, time-zones.md and controls.md. A skeleton laid down by Group A (the foundation); each group adds the rules of its own screens, and the lead settles the numbering.

1. **The rules are in `src/lib/agreements/`, one file each, every amount in minor units and every day a day key through `src/lib/time`**: `terms.js` (the terms' shape for the five kinds), `endDate.js`, `pricing.js`, `upfront.js`, `settling.js`, `coolingOff.js`, `standardWording.js` and `clauses.js`, `differences.js`, `quote.js`, `fx.js` (with `currency.js`), `renewals.js`, `hours.js`, `sessionOutcome.js`, `delivered.js`, `orgInvoice.js`, `needsYou.js`, `talkingPoints.js`, `status.js`. No page works out a price, a date, a clause, a difference or a line for itself. Each is checked by `tests/performance/agreements-<name>.test.mjs` with the brief's and M28's worked examples. **Polish 1 (Stage C, 26 Nov):** two more files: `questions.js` (the builder's questions, their words, the "So far:" line, what each answer changes, `emailWords`) and `places.js` (any city or country typed, giving its time zone and the time there now). `endDate.js` adds momentum rhythms and `sessionsInTerm`; `fx.js` adds `localFigures`, `localFor` and `underWarning` (the figure the client sees and the calm warning under the £ price); `settling.js` adds `equalCount` (monthly instalments follow a monthly term).
2. **The wording is code** (`standardWording.js`, version 0 of every clause), word for word from M28's STD and clauses() and the brief's items 11, 15, 38 and 39, with each agreement's notice hours and due days filled in. A paragraph may carry a reason, shown in a quieter grey line under it, indented, **with no side line** (risk 3; CLAUDE.md rule 4a), on screen and in the PDF. Clauses are numbered in order with no gaps. Clients invest and settle: "pay" never appears in client text (Apple Pay is a product name; a test reads every string). The paragraphs marked CLAUDE in `standardWording.js` (a block of hours' The work, a team's coaching and investment lines, and the confidentiality line "You hear when coaching starts and ends…") are Claude's, written where M28 draws none, for Tim to approve.
3. **A coach's own standard is a new version** (065's `agreement_clause_standards`: insert only, the owner's, read by every coach of the practice); an agreement's own edits are its `overrides`, each linked to the standard it changed; a version sent never changes, and every signed version keeps its full text as signed. Where the client chooses how to settle, the investment clause is stored with both texts (`choices`) and the signing record says which was chosen (`settle_choice`).
4. **Up front earns 5% off, automatically**, shown first and chosen to start with, switched off per agreement by the coach; never for a client funded by an organisation (`upfront.js`).
5. **The 14 days** (UK Consumer Contracts Regulations) are for self-funded individuals only (risk 18): the clause, the start-now tick (recorded with its time when ticked), no coaching session inside them without the tick (065's trigger, for client, coach and server alike: risk 29), and the coach's Cancel within 14 days (`coolingOff.js`'s `refund14`). The window runs to the end of the 14th day after the signing day; a session can be booked from the day after.
6. **Opened is the coach's only** (Tim, 25 Nov): first opens are recorded on the server as a link's page loads (065's `agreement_link_opens`), never by a third party, and `my_agreements` never carries them.
7. **One CLARITY- number run for everything** (Tim, 25 Nov; 064's `invoice_take_number`), organisations, as delivered, top-ups and first payments included; quotes have their own Q- run (065's `quote_take_number`), each taken at Send and never reused.
8. **No late-payment interest on any invoice** (Tim, 25 Nov; brief item 32).
9. **Any of M28's 15 currencies** (065's `currency_ok()`, `currency.js`; risk 8): the £ value beside the price at the daily reference rate with its day on hover, roughly what lands after Stripe's 2%, and a calm warning on what lands under the package's £ price with Match my £ price (risk 9), never blocking sending. A deposit rounds to the nearest 100 units of its currency, ¥10,000 for yen (risk 26).
10. **What the screens ask the server is one shape** (`src/lib/agreements/asks.js`, the page side `askClient.js`): POST `{ kind, ...body }` with the caller's own sign-in (none for a link's pages), answered `{ ok: true, … }` or `{ ok: false, message }` in calm words. A route not built yet answers 501, or isn't there at all (a plain 404: the dev server and a local build serve no api/), and a build that isn't the live site then takes the stand-in answer; the live site never does.
11. **The shared view parts** are `src/components/agreements/`: `AgreementView` (Main points, contents, clauses with reasons, an optional slot under each clause), `StepsMarker` (Read, Sign, Settle: the text colour for the current step, mint for done, never coral), `SettleChoice` (the shared InlineChoice; long choices wrap into rows), `SessionCheckRow` (a checked-off coaching session, "To confirm" when nobody has said yet, one click to its calendar pop-up), `agreements.css` (tokens only). **Polish 1 (Stage C, 26 Nov):** `StepsMarker` is no longer on the client's pages: Read, Sign and Settle run in focus mode on `ToolFrame`, its "Step n of m" and progress line (5e rule 29o's note); the 16 standard clauses' slot opens one clause at a time, and Ask for a change is Quick Add. `StepsMarker`, `AgreementView` and `SettleChoice` stay for the coach's Preview and the Needs you panels. The coach's own note has M28 v6's grey 1px quote line on its left (a hairline, not a coloured side line); reasons keep no line.

And the functionality tester's fixes (run 3, the lead's calls and the choices made with them): **one billing schedule** for every 065 agreement, `settling.js`'s `agreementSchedule` (built on `schedule`), read by the nightly draft (`coach-billing.js`), Finances' projections (`projected.js`), the first payment and Needs you alike; run 1's `invoicePlan`/`feeLines` only for versions made before 065 (no `terms.kind`), and a row that can't be read is left out of a page, never a crash. **A later signed version of a running agreement is an amendment** (a price review, an extension, a change): the first signing sets the schedule's shape (kind, way of settling, the client's choice, the first day), and the 14 days, the start-now tick and Cancel within 14 days always go by the first signing (065's `sign_agreement_v2` records no tick and keeps the first choice; `coach_sessions_065` reads the first signing; `my_agreements` says `amends`); an amendment makes no new first payment and goes straight to Welcome; its amounts apply to invoices due from its effective day, its signing day or its later `priceFrom`, whichever is later, the instalment numbers carrying on; while a newer version waits to be signed, the signed one keeps billing. **A retainer renews in 6-month terms**, drafted month after month until it is ended or not renewed, each line naming its month ("Leadership Sounding Board, January 2027"), never "of 6"; 6 months up front is the first invoice, then monthly from month 7. **One per-session price** (`delivered.js`'s `perSession`, for Needs you, the server, the 14-day refund and the settling words): set sessions, the plan's total without extras (5% off when up front was chosen) divided by its sessions, rounded down; a retainer, its fee divided by the coaching sessions a month it states (the beyond rate is for sessions beyond the package, so never this). **As delivered**, the first coaching session (a retainer: the first month's sessions) and each extra coaching session ticked are settled at signing, each extra at its own price, and are never invoiced again (`deliveredToInvoice`). **Ticked extras on a retainer, a one-off or a block of hours** are settled with the first payment, and a quote's total (`agreementTotal`) always includes them. **Organisation-funded agreements** (an individual's or a team's sponsor agreement) are invoiced to the organisation on the same schedule (no 5%, the first invoice on the signing day) by the nightly draft, to its billing profile's legal name and invoices email, with its payment terms (30 days without a profile) and the sponsor's, the terms' or the open purchase order; one held for a PO the profile needs can't be sent until one is added (Needs you's PO line); those clients' sessions never go on an hourly organisation invoice as well (`scheduledProfiles`). **Pauses:** an agreement's pause (`paused_from` to `paused_to`) moves every invoice due from its start on by its length; an early end (`ending_on` with `ending_by`) stops those after it. 061's reminder pauses (`invoice_reminder_pauses`) pause reminders only: invoices are still drafted, waiting for Send as ever (run 1's rule). **Cancel within 14 days** with the start-now tick refunds what was settled less what was delivered: sessions had at the per-session price (set sessions, a retainer), hours logged at the block's rate, or, for a one-off, the part the coach types as delivered (nothing typed: a full refund); only the agreement's own credit notes count; coaching sessions still to come come off the calendar as its own Delete takes them, with no email, and the coach is told to let the client know. **Needs you's first payment line** shows only while the agreement runs and its first invoice isn't settled; the nightly round never drafts one for an amendment or a cancelled agreement. **My hours** counts each top-up invoiced, as the coach's page does. **The client's Invoices** downloads each invoice's PDF from the server (`invoice_pdf`) and lists credit notes with theirs. **An organisation's evidence that can't be read stops the invoice** in calm words before anything is numbered; Invoice sent names the PDF as it was attached; Welcome opened by its address goes back to Settle while the first payment isn't settled (a stand-in answer never sends anyone back). And the break-it tester's fixes (run 3, the choices made with them): **an agreement cancelled within 14 days, run out or ended is never settled again**: the server's start, Direct Debit and state refuse it (`ended`), and Settle and Welcome say so in one calm line with Open My coaching (my_agreements carries `agreement_status`). **An amendment counts as settled only once the first signing's first invoice is paid**; until then the first payment is due by the first signing's own terms and choice (Settle makes it, Welcome sends the client there, the nightly round drafts the reminder on the version signed now, and Needs you's line and the reminder's amount follow). **061's `sign_agreement` refuses a builder version** (terms.kind), and a cancelled, run-out or waiting version; the client's fallback signs with `sign_agreement_v2`; the 14-day booking guard treats a builder version's start-now left empty as not ticked. **The 14-day guard covers every kind of session with the client except a chemistry call** (allowed by design; an "other" session is coaching time too). **A coach moves an agreement's status only from draft to sent**; signing, cancelling, running out, reopening and ending are their own steps on the server. **An organisation is never invoiced for sessions or time a self-funded agreement covers**: any signed one, whatever its status, from its start (or first signing, if earlier) to the end it has now (a run 1 agreement with no end is left as it was), on the New invoice page and in the server's send alike; the sessions settled at signing are recorded against the first payment in `invoice_sources` when the as-delivered invoice is drafted (a block of hours' time is covered by the same range). **The server makes the wording**: Send rebuilds a draft's clauses and total from its terms, own edits and the practice's standards (the builder's `versionFields`) and keeps them before it goes; an associate's draft that doesn't match is refused rather than changed after approval; the owner's Approve refuses wording its terms don't make, and the approval panel shows every stored clause in full. **Approval is of one version exactly as it stands** (065's `approved_version` and `approved_print`, a print of the terms, wording, total, edits, currency and funder): an associate's builder version is sent, quoted or accepted from its quote only once the owner approved that very version, unchanged since (`_agreement_send_ok`, in the guard and in `quote_accept`; the server's Send checks the same); an associate's price review waits for the owner, signed and asked; run 1's versions (no terms.kind) keep 061's rule. **A quote accepted with a choice** stores the investment clause in that choice (both texts kept for the signing block), the client's pages start on it (`choiceFor`), and `sign_agreement_v2` defaults to it. **A quote is never sent to someone their organisation funds** (CheckSend doesn't offer it and the server refuses), and `quote_open` never returns money for a funded version anyway. **A quote's wording on Accept is as it was sent** (the standards as they stood at its sending), **its question limit is held in the database** (`agreement_quotes.last_question_at`, the server's, one a minute, released when the email is refused), and **the quote's own coach opening their link isn't counted as Opened**.
