# Agreements, quotes and invoicing (Phase 6b run 3, 25 Nov 2026; mock-up M28 version 4; migration 065)

The run's brief (`phase-6b-run-3.md`, 46 items) wins over M28 where they differ, and these rules win over both. Read with coach-portal.md (rules 5, 9, 11, 22, 49, 50), pdf.md, emails.md, time-zones.md and controls.md. A skeleton laid down by Group A (the foundation); each group adds the rules of its own screens, and the lead settles the numbering.

1. **The rules are in `src/lib/agreements/`, one file each, every amount in minor units and every day a day key through `src/lib/time`**: `terms.js` (the terms' shape for the five kinds), `endDate.js`, `pricing.js`, `upfront.js`, `settling.js`, `coolingOff.js`, `standardWording.js` and `clauses.js`, `differences.js`, `quote.js`, `fx.js` (with `currency.js`), `renewals.js`, `hours.js`, `sessionOutcome.js`, `delivered.js`, `orgInvoice.js`, `needsYou.js`, `talkingPoints.js`, `status.js`. No page works out a price, a date, a clause, a difference or a line for itself. Each is checked by `tests/performance/agreements-<name>.test.mjs` with the brief's and M28's worked examples.
2. **The wording is code** (`standardWording.js`, version 0 of every clause), word for word from M28's STD and clauses() and the brief's items 11, 15, 38 and 39, with each agreement's notice hours and due days filled in. A paragraph may carry a reason, shown in a quieter grey line under it, indented, **with no side line** (risk 3; CLAUDE.md rule 4a), on screen and in the PDF. Clauses are numbered in order with no gaps. Clients invest and settle: "pay" never appears in client text (Apple Pay is a product name; a test reads every string). The paragraphs marked CLAUDE in `standardWording.js` (a block of hours' The work, a team's coaching and investment lines, and the confidentiality line "You hear when coaching starts and ends…") are Claude's, written where M28 draws none, for Tim to approve.
3. **A coach's own standard is a new version** (065's `agreement_clause_standards`: insert only, the owner's, read by every coach of the practice); an agreement's own edits are its `overrides`, each linked to the standard it changed; a version sent never changes, and every signed version keeps its full text as signed. Where the client chooses how to settle, the investment clause is stored with both texts (`choices`) and the signing record says which was chosen (`settle_choice`).
4. **Up front earns 5% off, automatically**, shown first and chosen to start with, switched off per agreement by the coach; never for a client funded by an organisation (`upfront.js`).
5. **The 14 days** (UK Consumer Contracts Regulations) are for self-funded individuals only (risk 18): the clause, the start-now tick (recorded with its time when ticked), no coaching session inside them without the tick (065's trigger, for client, coach and server alike: risk 29), and the coach's Cancel within 14 days (`coolingOff.js`'s `refund14`). The window runs to the end of the 14th day after the signing day; a session can be booked from the day after.
6. **Opened is the coach's only** (Tim, 25 Nov): first opens are recorded on the server as a link's page loads (065's `agreement_link_opens`), never by a third party, and `my_agreements` never carries them.
7. **One CLARITY- number run for everything** (Tim, 25 Nov; 064's `invoice_take_number`), organisations, as delivered, top-ups and first payments included; quotes have their own Q- run (065's `quote_take_number`), each taken at Send and never reused.
8. **No late-payment interest on any invoice** (Tim, 25 Nov; brief item 32).
9. **Any of M28's 15 currencies** (065's `currency_ok()`, `currency.js`; risk 8): the £ value beside the price at the daily reference rate with its day on hover, roughly what lands after Stripe's 2%, and a calm warning on what lands under the package's £ price with Match my £ price (risk 9), never blocking sending. A deposit rounds to the nearest 100 units of its currency, ¥10,000 for yen (risk 26).
10. **What the screens ask the server is one shape** (`src/lib/agreements/asks.js`, the page side `askClient.js`): POST `{ kind, ...body }` with the caller's own sign-in (none for a link's pages), answered `{ ok: true, … }` or `{ ok: false, message }` in calm words. A route not built yet answers 501, or isn't there at all (a plain 404: the dev server and a local build serve no api/), and a build that isn't the live site then takes the stand-in answer; the live site never does.
11. **The shared view parts** are `src/components/agreements/`: `AgreementView` (Main points, contents, clauses with reasons, an optional slot under each clause), `StepsMarker` (Read, Sign, Settle: the text colour for the current step, mint for done, never coral), `SettleChoice` (the shared InlineChoice; long choices wrap into rows), `SessionCheckRow` (a checked-off coaching session, "To confirm" when nobody has said yet, one click to its calendar pop-up), `agreements.css` (tokens only).

and Group C's and E's: Apple Pay as a card wallet in the Payment Element (no Link, no Google Pay), a quote's question emailed to the coach (one a minute per quote). And the functionality tester's fixes (run 3, the lead's calls and the choices made with them): **one billing schedule** for every 065 agreement, `settling.js`'s `agreementSchedule` (built on `schedule`), read by the nightly draft (`coach-billing.js`), Finances' projections (`projected.js`), the first payment and Needs you alike; run 1's `invoicePlan`/`feeLines` only for versions made before 065 (no `terms.kind`), and a row that can't be read is left out of a page, never a crash. **A later signed version of a running agreement is an amendment** (a price review, an extension, a change): the first signing sets the schedule's shape (kind, way of settling, the client's choice, the first day), and the 14 days, the start-now tick and Cancel within 14 days always go by the first signing (065's `sign_agreement_v2` records no tick and keeps the first choice; `coach_sessions_065` reads the first signing; `my_agreements` says `amends`); an amendment makes no new first payment and goes straight to Welcome; its amounts apply to invoices due from its effective day, its signing day or its later `priceFrom`, whichever is later, the instalment numbers carrying on; while a newer version waits to be signed, the signed one keeps billing. **A retainer renews in 6-month terms**, drafted month after month until it is ended or not renewed, each line naming its month ("Leadership Sounding Board, January 2027"), never "of 6"; 6 months up front is the first invoice, then monthly from month 7. **One per-session price** (`delivered.js`'s `perSession`, for Needs you, the server, the 14-day refund and the settling words): set sessions, the plan's total without extras (5% off when up front was chosen) divided by its sessions, rounded down; a retainer, its fee divided by the coaching sessions a month it states (the beyond rate is for sessions beyond the package, so never this). **As delivered**, the first coaching session (a retainer: the first month's sessions) and each extra coaching session ticked are settled at signing, each extra at its own price, and are never invoiced again (`deliveredToInvoice`). **Ticked extras on a retainer, a one-off or a block of hours** are settled with the first payment, and a quote's total (`agreementTotal`) always includes them. **Organisation-funded agreements** (an individual's or a team's sponsor agreement) are invoiced to the organisation on the same schedule (no 5%, the first invoice on the signing day) by the nightly draft, to its billing profile's legal name and invoices email, with its payment terms (30 days without a profile) and the sponsor's, the terms' or the open purchase order; one held for a PO the profile needs can't be sent until one is added (Needs you's PO line); those clients' sessions never go on an hourly organisation invoice as well (`scheduledProfiles`). **Pauses:** an agreement's pause (`paused_from` to `paused_to`) moves every invoice due from its start on by its length; an early end (`ending_on` with `ending_by`) stops those after it. 061's reminder pauses (`invoice_reminder_pauses`) pause reminders only: invoices are still drafted, waiting for Send as ever (run 1's rule). **Cancel within 14 days** with the start-now tick refunds what was settled less what was delivered: sessions had at the per-session price (set sessions, a retainer), hours logged at the block's rate, or, for a one-off, the part the coach types as delivered (nothing typed: a full refund); only the agreement's own credit notes count; coaching sessions still to come come off the calendar as its own Delete takes them, with no email, and the coach is told to let the client know. **Needs you's first payment line** shows only while the agreement runs and its first invoice isn't settled; the nightly round never drafts one for an amendment or a cancelled agreement. **My hours** counts each top-up invoiced, as the coach's page does. **The client's Invoices** downloads each invoice's PDF from the server (`invoice_pdf`) and lists credit notes with theirs. **An organisation's evidence that can't be read stops the invoice** in calm words before anything is numbered; Invoice sent names the PDF as it was attached; Welcome opened by its address goes back to Settle while the first payment isn't settled (a stand-in answer never sends anyone back).

13. **The builder is one page for the five kinds** (`/coach/agreements/:id/edit`, `Builder.jsx`), M28's parts in M28's order; the version's terms are kept on the device at once and sent through the store a moment after the last change ("Saved" in the meta line). Set sessions is the wide page with the underlined tabs row (jumps to each part) and "As [name] sees it" beside the form from 1100px; the other kinds, as M28 draws them, have neither. The pure rules are `src/lib/coach/agreementBuilder.js` (tests/performance/coach-builder.test.mjs): the end date follows the start, blocks and How often until the coach picks one; switching kind keeps who it is for, the terms and the price typed so far.
14. **Check before sending lists every difference** (`differences.js`), each with Edit (a clause opens that clause; the rest the builder), the Main points, what is settled at signing, and "How should it go?": an agreement to sign or a quote first (14, 30 or 60 days). The coach signs first (the saved signature, or one drawn or typed, kept in 061's signatures); the version is saved with its clauses (both settling texts where the client chooses), rate and note, then `agreements.send` is asked.
15. **An associate's agreement waits** (risk 23): signing it sets the version's coach signature and the agreement's approval to `waiting`; nothing is asked of the server until the owner approves.
16. **"Use this wording for everyone" adds a new standard version** (065, insert only), with the agreement's own figures turned back into `{noticeHours}` and `{dueDays}`; its Undo adds the earlier wording as the next version. Only the owner sets the standard; an associate reads it. "Just for [name]" keeps an override linked to the standard it changed.
17. **A package's own defaults** are edited in the same builder with no client (`/coach/agreements/<package id>/edit`, owner only), with its note ([name] filled in for each client).
18. **Off the live site the builder's send writes the sent state itself** after the stand-in answers (`sentRows`), so the flow can be followed on the dev server and the local stand-in; the live site never does.
19. **Needs you sits on Today above Who needs attention** (`src/pages/coach/needsYou/`), its facts from `src/lib/agreements/duringAgreement.js` (`needsYouItems`) and its words from `needsYou.js`, in M28's order. Every item drafts the next step in a side panel and sends nothing until its button is pressed; Done and Not now leave the list with Undo (065's `needs_you_done`; Not now is tomorrow, or next week for a renewal, a price review or the pound moving). A sent email can't be taken back, so a send says what went without Undo. While only the first screen is here it waits on its own crosshair (coach rule 49).
20. **One overdue invoice is never listed twice on Today**: an overdue invoice with its reminder drafted and waiting is in Needs you (`overdueInNeedsYou`; Today.jsx hands Who needs attention the rest through `attentionInvoices`); Who needs attention keeps the reminders that may have gone (Send again) and overdue invoices with no reminder waiting.
21. **Talking points show on their own moments** (the renewal and the price review), filled with that agreement's facts and the coach's own words from Settings; they are for the coach to say and never sent.
22. **What happened?** opens from the calendar for a client's coaching session that is over and not said yet (and from any agreement's or invoice's session row through `/coach/calendar?item=<id>`): It happened, Cancelled late (counts as used), Rearranged (not counted), Not sure yet, saved through the calendar's one save with Undo; No-show stays on the session's page.
23. **An agreement's page** (`/coach/agreements/:id`) holds every change to a running agreement; Cancel within 14 days shows only for a self-funded individual inside their 14 days (`coolingOff.offered`), never for an organisation's or a team's.
24. **An organisation's invoice** comes from what's logged since its last invoice (none already on a live invoice), at the profile's or sponsor's hourly rate (changeable for one invoice), and won't send while a PO or evidence is missing, each in calm words (`orgInvoice.problems`); the one PDF is the server's, previewed as itself.
25. **The end a running agreement has now** is `agreements.ends_on` when Extend or Pause moved it (065; written only by the server, which 065's guard enforces), else `ending_on` with no `ending_by`, else the signed `terms.endsOn` (`duringAgreement.currentEnd`, and the server's own `currentEnd`); an early end is `ending_on` with `ending_by` (`earlyEnd`).
