# Claims register: the evidence behind what the portal says

Version 1.1, written 26 Oct 2026 by Claude for Tim (Dr. Tim Dutton, The Clarity Project LTD); audited the same evening to the citation standard Tim set at 18:47 (Part C).

Every claim the portal makes, every score's basis, and every tool, with how strong the evidence is and where it comes from (Tim, 26 Oct, 16:47 to 16:54). It sits beside the methods document (claude/methods/scoring-methods.md), which says how each score is worked out. The coach portal's evidence library is built from this register, and Tim adds to it there.

## How strength is rated

| Strength | Means | Said to clients as |
|---|---|---|
| Strong | Several good reviews of many studies agree, or an official guideline | Plain statement with its references on tap |
| Moderate | One good review, or several good studies, with some caveats | Plain statement, with the caveat on tap |
| Early | A few or small studies, mostly in the lab or from surveys | "Early evidence suggests…" |
| Our model | A coaching model or practitioner framework with little direct research | "Our model" or "A coaching approach used by…" |

Rules (Tim, 26 Oct):
- Weak or mixed evidence is said plainly, never dressed up.
- Every claim marked Early or Our model is flagged to Tim in the evidence library.
- Strength is about the idea behind a claim or tool. It is never a claim that the portal's own version has been tested, which it hasn't yet.
- Wording in the portal never goes further than the evidence. For example, "Slow breathing can help settle your body", not "Breathing cures stress".
- The portal never claims that coaching or the portal caused an organisation's results (methods document M19.5).
